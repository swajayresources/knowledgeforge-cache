This study investigates the effect of indirect p-type co-doping on the magnetic properties of (Ga,Mn)N thin films. The researchers grew trilayer heterostructures with varying Mg doping levels and found that the presence of neighboring GAN:Mg layers did not affect the spin-spin interactions in the central (Ga,Mn)N layer. The results show that the layers exhibit purely paramagnetic response at temperatures above 50 K, with no effect of the Mg doping.
