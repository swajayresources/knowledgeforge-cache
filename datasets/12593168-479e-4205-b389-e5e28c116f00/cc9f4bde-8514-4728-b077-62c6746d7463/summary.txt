This paper reports the growth and characterization of all-wurtzite (In,Ga)As-(Ga,Mn)As core-shell nanowires by molecular beam epitaxy. The nanowires exhibit a high-quality wurtzite crystal structure with smooth (Ga,Mn)As shells containing 5% Mn epitaxially deposited on (In,Ga)As cores with 10% In. Magnetic studies indicate the presence of ferromagnetic coupling in the (Ga,Mn)As shells at temperatures above 33 K, though the overall behavior is superparamagnetic.
