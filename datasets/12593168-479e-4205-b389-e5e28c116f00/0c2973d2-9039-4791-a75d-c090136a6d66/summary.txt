This paper investigates the magnetic properties of (Ga,Mn)N thin films in proximity to Mg-doped GaN using improved-sensitivity integral SQUID magnetometry. The results show that the presence of neighboring GaN:Mg layers does not affect the spin-spin interactions in the central (Ga,Mn)N layer. However, the lack of any effect of the adjacent Mg-doping is attributed to the presence of residual Mn in the cladding layers.
