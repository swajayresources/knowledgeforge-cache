This paper investigates the anisotropy of high temperature strength in a precipitation-hardened nickel-base superalloy single crystal, Alloy 454. The results show marked anisotropy in tensile yield strength, creep, and creep rupture strength at around 760°C, with orientations near the -111 and 011 axes exhibiting extremely low strength compared to the 001 axis. The anisotropic behavior is explained in terms of the available slip systems and the stacking geometry of the gamma-prime precipitates.
