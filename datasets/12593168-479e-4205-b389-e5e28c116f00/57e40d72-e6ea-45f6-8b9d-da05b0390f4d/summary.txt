This paper presents D-v2v, a new dynamic (one-pass) variable-to-variable text compressor. D-v2v processes the input text word-wise, gathering variable-length symbols that can be either single words or sequences of words seen before in the text. It uses a Patricia tree-like structure to efficiently handle the variable-length sequences and encodes the symbols using dense codes to achieve competitive compression ratios compared to state-of-the-art compressors.
