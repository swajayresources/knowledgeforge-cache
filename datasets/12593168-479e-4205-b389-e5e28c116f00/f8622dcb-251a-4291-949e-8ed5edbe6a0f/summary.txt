This paper presents the design of a surface trap for creating freely rotating ion ring crystals, with the goal of trapping around 100 ions in a small ring structure of 100-200 μm diameter. The authors analyze various imperfections that could break the rotational symmetry, such as external electric fields, electrode edge irregularities, and local charging, and conclude that these can be sufficiently controlled to allow for freely rotating ion rings even at energies comparable to the ground state.
