This paper proposes a novel two-dimensional nonlinear tuned mass damper inerter (2d-NTMDI) to mitigate bi-directional structural responses, particularly in wind turbine blades. The 2d-NTMDI consists of a mass and two sets of springs, dashpots, and inerters configured in two axes, resulting in a nonlinear restoring force. The proposed device is effective in reducing bi-directional vibrations and prolonging the fatigue life of wind turbine blades by 35%.
