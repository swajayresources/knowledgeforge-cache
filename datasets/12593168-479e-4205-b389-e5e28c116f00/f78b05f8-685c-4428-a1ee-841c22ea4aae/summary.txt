This paper investigates the binary phase diagram of the CaF2-SrF2 system using differential thermal analysis (DTA). It finds that CaF2 and SrF2 exhibit unlimited mutual solubility with an azeotropic point at a composition of Ca0.582Sr0.418F2 and a minimum melting temperature of 1373°C. Single crystals of this composition can be grown by the Czochralski method without significant segregation.
