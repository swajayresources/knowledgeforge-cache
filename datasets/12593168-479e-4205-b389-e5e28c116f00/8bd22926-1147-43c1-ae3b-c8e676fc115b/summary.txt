This paper investigates the local crystallographic structure of ferromagnetic γ'-Ga_y Fe_4-y N nanocrystals embedded in GaN thin layers using a combination of anomalous X-ray diffraction and diffraction anomalous fine structure. The results show that Ga atoms preferentially occupy the 3c Wyckoff sites in the fcc structure of the nanocrystals, and the magnetic properties of the nanocrystals are inversely correlated with the amount of Ga in the 3c sites.
