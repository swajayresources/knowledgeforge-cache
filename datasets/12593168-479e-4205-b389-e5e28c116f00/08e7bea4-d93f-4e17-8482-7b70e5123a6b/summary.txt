This paper explores the effects of isotope composition on Alfvén eigenmode (AE) stability in the JET tokamak, using a database of over 1,000 AE stability measurements. The results show that AE damping rates decrease with increasing effective ion mass, but increase for helium plasmas. The study also demonstrates the use of active MHD spectroscopy to infer the effective ion mass and isotope ratio in hydrogenic plasmas.
