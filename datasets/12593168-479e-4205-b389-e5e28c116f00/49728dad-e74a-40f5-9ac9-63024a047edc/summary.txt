The paper studies a robust optimal stopping problem with respect to a set of mutually singular probabilities, which can be interpreted as a zero-sum controller-stopper game. The authors show that the upper Snell envelope of the reward process is a supermartingale with respect to an appropriately defined nonlinear expectation, and it is further an E-martingale up to the first time it meets the reward process, which is the optimal stopping time for the robust optimal stopping problem.
