This paper presents thermal-structural mission analyses of air-cooled gas turbine blades, including all-impingement cooled and leading-edge film-cooled configurations. The analyses used advanced 3D finite element methods to calculate cyclic temperatures, stresses, strains, and predicted creep lives under a simulated engine mission profile. The results show that creep is the dominant damage mode, with leading-edge film cooling increasing the creep strain range compared to all-impingement cooling.
