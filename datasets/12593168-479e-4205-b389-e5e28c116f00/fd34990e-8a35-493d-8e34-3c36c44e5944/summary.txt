This paper investigates the brachistochrone problem for a fluid-filled cylinder, where the goal is to find the curve that a fluid-filled bottle would follow in the shortest time to reach a given endpoint. The authors use optimal control theory and numerical methods to solve the problem and find that the brachistochrone deviates from a cycloid due to viscous dissipation.
