This paper investigates the thermal conductivity of zirconia-yttria and BSAS/mullite-based thermal barrier and environmental barrier coating materials using a steady-state laser heat flux technique. The results show that the thermal conductivity of these coatings ranges from 1.0 to 3.0 W/m-K, with the plasma-sprayed coatings having lower initial conductivity values due to higher porosity, but increasing over time due to sintering.
