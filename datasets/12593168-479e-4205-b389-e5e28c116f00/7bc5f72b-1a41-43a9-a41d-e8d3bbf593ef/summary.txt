This paper investigates the thermomechanical fatigue (TMF) failure of a single crystal nickel superalloy turbine blade. A specialized TMF test rig was developed to simulate the complex thermal and mechanical loads experienced by turbine blades. The tested specimen was carefully segmented to preserve the crack surfaces, which were then analyzed using SEM to identify the TMF failure mechanism. Finite element simulation was also conducted to validate the location of most severe TMF damage.
