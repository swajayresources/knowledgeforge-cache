This paper presents a new approach to understanding the function of open channels in proteins and channels, using a physical model of electrodiffusion and the nonlinear Poisson-Nernst-Planck (PNP) equations. The PNP theory can predict the current through an open channel given its structure and distribution of fixed charge. This approach has been shown to fit a wide range of data and can be used to understand the behavior of channels and enzymes.
