This paper analyzes the universality of the ion flux to the JET outer wall across a wide range of plasma current and density conditions. It finds that the probability distribution functions (PDFs) of the ion saturation current measured by an outer wall-mounted Langmuir probe collapse onto a single curve after normalization, indicating universal behavior.
