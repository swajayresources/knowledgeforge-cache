This paper presents the structural optimization of two existing turbine blade designs, the low-pressure T106C and high-pressure C3X (cooled) turbine blades, using two topology optimization methods: the Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization (SIMP) and the Sequential Element Rejections and Admissions (SERA) methods. The results show that the SERA method produced better-performing geometries with 24.7% and 37.9% lower objective functions for the T106C and C3X geometries, respectively, compared
