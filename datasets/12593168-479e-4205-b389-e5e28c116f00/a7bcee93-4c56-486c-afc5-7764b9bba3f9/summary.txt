This paper investigates the electrical characteristics of a vertical-geometry Schottky junction to magnetic insulator (Ga,Mn)N heteroepitaxially grown on sapphire. The study finds that the junction exhibits a high resistance of over 10 MΩ at room temperature, indicating nearly conductive-dislocation-free electrical properties. The results suggest that the (Ga,Mn)N layer plays a crucial role in achieving these properties.
