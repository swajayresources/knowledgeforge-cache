This paper presents a new classification system, Integrated Interval Mahalanobis Classification System (IIMCS), for classifying complex metal parts based on their broadband vibration response. The IIMCS system addresses the challenges of measurement uncertainties in the context of the Mahalanobis-Taguchi System (MTS). The system is demonstrated in an experimental case study of an equiaxed nickel alloy first-stage turbine blade, showing satisfactory performance.
