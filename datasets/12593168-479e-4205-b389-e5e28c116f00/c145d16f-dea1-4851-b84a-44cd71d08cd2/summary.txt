This paper analyzes Alfvénic activity observed in JET plasmas, which is explained by a model coupling shear-Alfvén and acoustic continua that depends strongly on plasma shaping, particularly elongation. The model predicts high-order geodesic acoustic eigenmodes (HOGAEs) with frequencies close to one-half of the typical toroidicity-induced Alfvén eigenmode (TAE) frequency, which are found to agree with experimental measurements.
