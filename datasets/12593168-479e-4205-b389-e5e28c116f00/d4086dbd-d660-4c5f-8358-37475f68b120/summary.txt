This paper investigates the magnetic properties of (Ga,Mn)N thin films in proximity to Mg-doped GaN using improved-sensitivity integral SQUID magnetometry. The results show that the presence of neighboring GaN:Mg layers does not affect the spin-spin interactions in the central (Ga,Mn)N layer. The study concludes that a more intensive technological effort is needed to break the deadlock in the effectiveness of simultaneous Mn- and Mg-codoping in GaN.
