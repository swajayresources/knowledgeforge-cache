This paper discusses the development of thermal barrier coatings for the Space Shuttle Main Engine (SSME) turbine blades, which experience severe thermal shocks during operation. The researchers tested various coatings using a plasma spraying technique and found that a 50-50 blend of Cr2O3 and NiCrAlY, vacuum plasma sprayed on SSME turbopump turbine blades, provided significant improvements in coating durability and thermal protection.
