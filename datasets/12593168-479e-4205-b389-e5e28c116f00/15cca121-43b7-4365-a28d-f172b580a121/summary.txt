This paper investigates the microstructural characteristics of a nickel-based superalloy (AM1) under high temperature fatigue at 950°C. It identifies two main types of deformation microstructures - anisotropic and homogeneous - and maps them as a function of the number of cycles and total strain amplitude. The study highlights the role of internal stresses, diffusion processes, and precipitate shearing in governing the evolution of the microstructures.
