This paper uses contactless electroreflectance spectroscopy to determine the Fermi level position and band offsets in insulating (Ga,Mn)N/GaN structures. The results show that the Fermi level is strongly pinned in the middle of the band gap in (Ga,Mn)N, and most of the band gap change occurs in the valence band, with a shift of -0.028 eV per % Mn. These findings have implications for the use of (Ga,Mn)N as an insulating buffer layer in nitride devices.
