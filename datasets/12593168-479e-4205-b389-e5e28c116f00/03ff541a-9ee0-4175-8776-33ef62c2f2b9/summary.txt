This paper investigates the orientation and temperature dependence of the mechanical properties of the single-crystal nickel-base superalloy Rene N4, with a focus on tension-compression anisotropy. The results show that primary cube slip occurred even at room temperature for some orientations, and the range of orientations exhibiting cube slip expanded with increasing temperature.
