This paper proposes the Integrated Interval Mahalanobis Classification System (IIMCS) for classifying complex metal parts based on their broadband vibration response, addressing the challenges of measurement uncertainties in the Mahalanobis-Taguchi System (MTS). The IIMCS employs a Monte Carlo method framework to propagate measurement uncertainty into the Mahalanobis distance space and assigns an indicator to evaluate classification reliability.
