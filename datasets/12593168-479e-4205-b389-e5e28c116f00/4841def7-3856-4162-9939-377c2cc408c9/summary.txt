This paper presents a novel CMOS highly parallel, low-power, multi-chip neural network accelerator called NV-1, which was co-designed with a digital twin approach to ensure efficient hardware implementation. The NV-1 chip has 3200 cores, achieves 447 GB/s memory bandwidth, and consumes very low power (<10 mW). The architecture is designed for massively parallel software and can be scaled up to huge arrays for applications such as server farms.
