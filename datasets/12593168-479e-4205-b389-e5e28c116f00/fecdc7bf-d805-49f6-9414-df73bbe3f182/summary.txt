This paper investigates the local crystallographic structure of ferromagnetic Ga_yFe_4-yN nanocrystals embedded in GaN using a combination of anomalous x-ray diffraction and diffraction anomalous fine structure. The results show that Ga atoms occupy 3c sites in the fcc structure of the nanocrystals, and the amount of Ga in 3c sites increases with the normalized Ga-flow.
