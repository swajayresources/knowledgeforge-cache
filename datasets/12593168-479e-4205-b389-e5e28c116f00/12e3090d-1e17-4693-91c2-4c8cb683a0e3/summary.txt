This paper demonstrates the existence of a 'frozen mode' in an asymmetric serpentine optical waveguide (ASOW), which exhibits vanishing group velocity and enhanced field amplitude. The frozen mode is associated with a stationary inflection point (SIP) in the Bloch dispersion relation, where three Bloch eigenmodes coalesce. The authors derive design equations to realize the SIP and analyze the scattering properties of a finite-length ASOW supporting the frozen mode.
