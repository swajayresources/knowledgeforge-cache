This paper investigates the local crystallographic structure of ferromagnetic Ga_y Fe_4-y N nanocrystals embedded in GaN thin layers using a combination of anomalous X-ray diffraction and diffraction anomalous fine structure. The results show that Ga atoms preferentially occupy the 3c Wyckoff sites in the face-centered cubic structure of the nanocrystals, and the magnetic properties of the nanocrystals are correlated with the amount of Ga incorporated.
