This paper investigates the temperature and orientation dependence of the anisotropy and creep behavior of a single-crystal nickel superalloy (Alloy 454). It finds that the alloy exhibits strong anisotropy in tensile strength and creep resistance at around 760°C, with orientations near the -111 and 011 axes showing much lower strength compared to the 001 axis.
