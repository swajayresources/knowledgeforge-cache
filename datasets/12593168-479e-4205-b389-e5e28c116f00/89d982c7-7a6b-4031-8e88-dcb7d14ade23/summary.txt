This paper investigates the bithermal fatigue behavior of a nickel-base superalloy single crystal, Rene N4, with and without a diffusion-aluminide coating. It finds that bithermal fatigue lives are shorter than isothermal fatigue lives at either temperature extreme when compared on an inelastic strain basis, and that the coating can be detrimental for isothermal fatigue at 760°C and out-of-phase fatigue under large strains.
