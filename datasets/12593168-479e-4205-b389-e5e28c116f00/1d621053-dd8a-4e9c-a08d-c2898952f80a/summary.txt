This paper proposes WaveletAE, a wavelet-enhanced autoencoder model for detecting wind turbine blade icing using multivariate time series data from the SCADA system. WaveletAE combines wavelet decomposition, convolutional encoders, and LSTM encoders to capture complex channel-wise correlations and temporal patterns across multiple scales, outperforming state-of-the-art anomaly detection methods on a real-world dataset.
