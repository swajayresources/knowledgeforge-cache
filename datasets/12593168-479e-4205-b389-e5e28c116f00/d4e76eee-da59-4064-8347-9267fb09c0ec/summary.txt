This paper investigates the transition from irregular to regular eutectic growth in the Al-Al3Ni system using in situ optical microscopy and synchrotron x-ray microscopy. The authors observe a morphological transition from a eutectic-grain dependent, irregular eutectic growth at low solidification velocities to a weakly anisotropic, regular growth at higher velocities, and correlate this with the shape and orientation of the Al3Ni intermetallic crystals.
