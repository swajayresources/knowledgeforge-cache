This paper presents direct numerical simulations (DNS) of a row of normal film cooling jets in a crossflow, with the goal of understanding the dynamics of the large-scale structures in the near-field region. The results reveal complex unsteady flow structures, including shear layer vortices, counter-rotating vortex pairs, horseshoe vortices, and wall vortices, which are influenced by a periodic interaction between the jet hole boundary layer and the approaching crossflow.
