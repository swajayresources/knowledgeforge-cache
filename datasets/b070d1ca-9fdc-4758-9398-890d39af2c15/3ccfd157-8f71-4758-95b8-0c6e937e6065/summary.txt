The paper presents D-v2v, a new dynamic (one-pass) variable-to-variable text compressor that processes the input text word-wise and gathers variable-length symbols representing sequences of words. D-v2v uses a Patricia tree-based structure to efficiently handle the subsequences seen before in the input, and encodes the symbols using dense codes.
