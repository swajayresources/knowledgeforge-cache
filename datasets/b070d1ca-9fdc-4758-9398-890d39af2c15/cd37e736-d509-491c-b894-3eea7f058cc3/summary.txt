This paper investigates the local crystallographic structure of ferromagnetic Ga-doped Fe-nitride (Ga_y Fe_4-y N) nanocrystals embedded in GaN thin films. Using a combination of anomalous X-ray diffraction and diffraction anomalous fine structure, the authors find that Ga atoms preferentially occupy the 3c Wyckoff sites in the face-centered cubic structure of the nanocrystals, with the amount of Ga incorporation increasing linearly with the Ga supply during growth.
