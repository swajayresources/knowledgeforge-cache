This paper presents a numerical study of an 'anti-vortex' film cooling design that aims to mitigate the adverse effects of jet vorticity and lift-off at high blowing ratios. The anti-vortex concept involves adding two branched holes from each primary hole to produce a vorticity counter to the detrimental kidney vortices. The computational results show that the modified anti-vortex design improves film cooling effectiveness compared to the baseline round hole configuration.
