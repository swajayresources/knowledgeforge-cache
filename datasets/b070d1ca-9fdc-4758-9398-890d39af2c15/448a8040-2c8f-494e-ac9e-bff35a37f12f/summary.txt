This paper presents an adaptation of Greenberg's potential flow model to predict the loads on vertical-axis wind turbine blades. The model is extended to handle multi-harmonic oscillations in effective angle of attack and incoming flow velocity, which are more representative of the blade kinematics at low tip-speed ratios.
