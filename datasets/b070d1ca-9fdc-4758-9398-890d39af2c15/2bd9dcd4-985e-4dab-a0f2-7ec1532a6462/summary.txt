This paper explores the use of multi-material coatings to reduce thermal noise and optimize optical absorption in cryogenic gravitational wave detectors. The authors demonstrate a coating design with low-absorbing Ta2O5 in the outer layers and higher-index, lower-loss amorphous silicon (aSi) in the inner layers, which can reduce Brownian thermal noise by 25% compared to standard SiO2/Ta2O5 coatings.
