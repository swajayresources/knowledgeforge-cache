This paper investigates the effect of hydrogen on the deformation structure and mechanical properties of the CMSX-2 nickel-base single-crystal superalloy. The study found that high-pressure, high-temperature hydrogen charging significantly increased work hardening, reduced uniform elongation, and decreased fatigue life compared to hydrogen-free material. Microscopic analysis revealed that hydrogen enhanced dislocation accumulation in the gamma matrix and promoted cross-slip of superdislocations
