This paper investigates the local crystallographic structure of ferromagnetic Ga-doped Fe-N nanocrystals embedded in GaN thin films using a combination of anomalous X-ray diffraction and diffraction anomalous fine structure. The results show that Ga preferentially occupies the 3c Wyckoff sites in the face-centered cubic γ'-Ga_y Fe_4-y N structure, and the magnetic properties of the nanocrystals are inversely correlated with the amount of Ga in the 3c sites.
