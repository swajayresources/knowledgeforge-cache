This paper describes the development and testing of a thermal barrier coating (TBC) system for use on gas turbine engine components. The TBC system consisting of a Ni-Cr-Al-Y bond coat and a Zr02-20%Y203 ceramic top coat was selected from 8 candidate coating systems based on extensive laboratory testing. The selected TBC was applied to CF6-50 high pressure turbine blades and vanes and evaluated through cascade rig testing, component fatigue testing, and engine testing.
