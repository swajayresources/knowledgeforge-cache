This paper assesses the use of additive manufacturing (3D printing) for the design and fabrication of a rotor fixture used in gas turbine assembly. The researchers used finite element analysis and topology optimization to redesign the fixture, achieving a 16.3% mass reduction for a solid model and 27.5% for a shell model while maintaining stiffness. They also found that metal additive manufacturing reduced lead time by 46% compared to conventional machining, despite a 17% increase in cost.
