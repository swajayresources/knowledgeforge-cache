This paper investigates the use of silicon nitride (Si3N4) and silicon carbide/silicon carbide (SiC/SiC) materials for turbine engine components, focusing on the development of thermal barrier coatings (TBCs) and environmental barrier coatings (EBCs) to improve engine efficiency. The paper presents finite element analysis (FEA) and nondestructive evaluation (NDE) of cooled silicon nitride plates, demonstrating the impact of cooling channel configuration on temperature distribution.
