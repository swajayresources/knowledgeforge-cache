This paper investigates the effect of titanium (Ti) on the oxidation behavior of a polycrystalline nickel-based superalloy, RR1000, using high-resolution characterization techniques such as atom probe tomography. The results show that Ti segregates primarily to the grain boundaries in the chromia scale, leading to enhanced outward diffusion of Ti and the formation of a titanium-rich rutile layer on the outer surface, which is detrimental to the oxidation resistance of the alloy.
