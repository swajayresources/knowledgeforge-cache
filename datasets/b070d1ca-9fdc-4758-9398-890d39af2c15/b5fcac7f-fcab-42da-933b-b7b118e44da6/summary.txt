This paper presents an adaptation of Greenberg's potential flow model to predict the loads on a vertical-axis wind turbine blade. The model is extended to handle multi-harmonic oscillations in effective angle of attack and incoming flow velocity, which are more representative of the blade kinematics at low tip-speed ratios. The results of the adapted model are compared with experimental data from a scaled-down model of a single-bladed H-type Darrieus wind turbine.
