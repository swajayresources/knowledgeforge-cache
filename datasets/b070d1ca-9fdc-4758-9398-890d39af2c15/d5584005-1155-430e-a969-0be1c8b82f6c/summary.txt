This paper investigates the temperature and crystallographic orientation dependence of the tensile strength, creep, and creep rupture strength of a single-crystal nickel superalloy (Alloy 454). The results show strong anisotropy at around 760°C, with orientations near the -111 and 011 axes exhibiting much lower strength compared to the 001 axis. The anisotropy is explained in terms of the available slip systems and the stacking geometry of the gamma-prime precipitates.
