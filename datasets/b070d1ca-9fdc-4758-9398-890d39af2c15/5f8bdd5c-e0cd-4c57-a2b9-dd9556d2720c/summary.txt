This paper analyzes Alfvénic activity observed in JET plasmas below the typical toroidicity-induced Alfvén eigenmode (TAE) frequency. The authors employ a model coupling the shear-Alfvén and acoustic continua, which depends strongly on plasma shaping and elongation, to explain the properties of these lower-frequency Alfvénic modes, referred to as high-order geodesic acoustic eigenmodes (HOGAEs).
