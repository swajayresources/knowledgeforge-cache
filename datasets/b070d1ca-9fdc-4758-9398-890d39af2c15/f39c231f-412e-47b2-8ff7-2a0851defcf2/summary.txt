This paper presents a combined experimental and computational study to investigate the erosion of thermal barrier coated (TBC) turbine blades by alumina particles. Experimental measurements were obtained for EB-PVD 7YSZ/PtAl coated Inconel 718 coupons to determine erosion rates and particle restitution characteristics under different impact conditions.
