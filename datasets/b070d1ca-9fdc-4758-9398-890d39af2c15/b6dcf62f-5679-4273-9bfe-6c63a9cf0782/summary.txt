This paper investigates the effects of homogenization treatment on the microsegregation of a Ni-Co based superalloy produced by directional solidification. The results show that severe microsegregation leads to complicated precipitations, including η-Ni3Ti and eutectic (γ + γ'). An optimal single-stage homogenization process of 1180°C for 2 hours is determined to eliminate dendritic segregation and improve the hot-working performance of the alloy.
