This paper investigates the transition from irregular to regular eutectic growth in the Al-Al3Ni system using in situ optical microscopy and synchrotron x-ray microscopy. The authors observe that at low solidification velocities (1 μm/s), the faceted Al3Ni phase grows ahead of the Al phase, leading to irregular eutectic patterns. As the velocity increases (up to 10 μm/s), the Al3Ni phase becomes less faceted and the growth transitions to a more regular, coupled rod-like structure.
