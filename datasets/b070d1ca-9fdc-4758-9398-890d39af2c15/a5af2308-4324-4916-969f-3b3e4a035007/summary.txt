This paper investigates the effect of the ITER-like wall (ILW) on runaway electron (RE) generation in JET disruptions. It compares two similar slow argon injection disruptions, one with a carbon wall and one with the ILW, and finds that the ILW case has a slower current quench and negligible runaway current, in contrast to the carbon wall case which exhibits a runaway electron plateau.
