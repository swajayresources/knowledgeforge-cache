This paper investigates the local crystallographic structure of ferromagnetic γ'-Ga₄₋ₓFeₓN nanocrystals embedded in a GaN matrix using a combination of anomalous X-ray diffraction and diffraction anomalous fine structure. The results show that Ga atoms preferentially occupy the 3c Wyckoff sites in the fcc structure of the nanocrystals, and the magnetic properties of the nanocrystals are inversely correlated with the amount of Ga incorporated.
