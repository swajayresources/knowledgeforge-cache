This paper reviews NASA's progress in developing ceramic thermal barrier coatings for industrial/utility gas turbines under the DOE-sponsored Critical Research and Advanced Technology Support Project. It discusses the sensitivity of zirconia-yttria coatings to sodium and vanadium fuel impurities, identifies new coating systems based on calcium silicate and magnesium-nickel chromium aluminum yttrium, and shows that coating life can be extended by reducing thickness and using more oxidation-resist
