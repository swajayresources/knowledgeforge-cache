This paper presents the design and optimization of a nacelle for an Ultra-High Bypass Ratio (UHBR) turbofan engine using an intuitive Class Shape Transformation (iCST) based parameterization. The optimization was performed separately for the intake-fan cowl and bypass duct-nozzle, and the final optimized design achieved a cruise drag of 26.9 drag counts with a maximum velocity coefficient of 0.9914 in the bypass nozzle.
