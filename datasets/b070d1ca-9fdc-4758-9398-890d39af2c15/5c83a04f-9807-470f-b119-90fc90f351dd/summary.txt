This paper presents the structural optimization of two existing turbine blade designs, the low-pressure T106C and high-pressure C3X (cooled) blades, using two topology optimization methods: Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization (SIMP) and Sequential Element Rejections and Admissions (SERA).
