This paper presents a new wireless and self-sufficient aerodynamic measurement system called Aerosense, which uses MEMS sensors to obtain local aerodynamic pressures, blade motions, and inflow conditions on wind turbine rotor blades. The system is demonstrated on a 7kW wind turbine, showing that the pressure distribution can vary significantly during one rotation of the blade, even under stable wind conditions, due to the misalignment of the wind direction with the wind turbine's rotational axis
