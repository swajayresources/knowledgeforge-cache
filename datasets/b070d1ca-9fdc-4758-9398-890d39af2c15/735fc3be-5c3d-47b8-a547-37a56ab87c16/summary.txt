This paper compares the design and off-design aerodynamic performance of a natural laminar airfoil, the SHM1, used in the Honda Jet. It employs numerical simulations to analyze the flow physics and aerodynamic performance across different Mach numbers in the subsonic and transonic regimes, examining design conditions like low speed, climb, and cruise, as well as off-design scenarios involving transonic shock emergence, drag-divergence, and shock-induced separation.
