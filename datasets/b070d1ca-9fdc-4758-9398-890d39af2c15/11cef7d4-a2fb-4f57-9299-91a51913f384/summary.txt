This paper investigates the bithermal fatigue behavior of a nickel-base superalloy single crystal, Rene N4, with and without a diffusion-aluminide coating. The bithermal fatigue lives of bare specimens were shorter than isothermal fatigue lives, and out-of-phase bithermal fatigue resulted in the shortest lives. The coating had a detrimental effect on fatigue life at 760°C and under large strains in out-of-phase bithermal fatigue.
