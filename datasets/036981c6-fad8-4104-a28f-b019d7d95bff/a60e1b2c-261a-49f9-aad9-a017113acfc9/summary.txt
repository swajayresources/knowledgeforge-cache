This paper theoretically proposes and numerically demonstrates a multilayered plasmonic shell cloak that reduces the total scattering cross section of a dielectric or conducting nanoparticle simultaneously at multiple optical frequencies. By exploiting the frequency dispersion and negative polarizability of plasmonic materials, the authors design a two-layered cloak that achieves drastic scattering reduction at two distinct wavelengths (500 nm and 625 nm), with potential extension to more layers
