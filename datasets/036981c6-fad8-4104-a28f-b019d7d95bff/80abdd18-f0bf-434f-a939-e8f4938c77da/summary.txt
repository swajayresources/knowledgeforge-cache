The paper introduces the concept of a mantle cloak, a thin patterned metasurface that can drastically reduce the electromagnetic scattering of an object, effectively rendering it invisible. Unlike traditional volumetric metamaterial cloaks that require finite thickness, the mantle cloak operates as a surface impedance layer, enabling simpler, thinner, and potentially broadband cloaking for dielectric and conducting objects.
