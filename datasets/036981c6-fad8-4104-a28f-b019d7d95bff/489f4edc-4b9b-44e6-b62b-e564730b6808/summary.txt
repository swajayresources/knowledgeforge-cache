This paper investigates the application of Rayleigh-Fourier and Rayleigh least-squares methods to electromagnetic wave scattering at periodic rough interfaces between general homogeneous media, including anisotropic and bi-isotropic materials. It demonstrates that the Rayleigh-Fourier method converges under the same conditions as the Waterman-Fourier method and that the Rayleigh least-squares method converges numerically when the solution is unique.
