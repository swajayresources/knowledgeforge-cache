This paper proposes a novel approach to broadband radar cross section (RCS) reduction by embedding a small number of short carbon fiber (CF) yarns into glass fabric composites, leveraging phase cancellation rather than absorption. The study demonstrates both numerically and experimentally that such composites achieve over 10 dB RCS reduction from 8.7 to 19.2 GHz with a thickness of 2.7 mm, using chessboard and random CF patterns, while maintaining mechanical robustness and simple fabrication.
