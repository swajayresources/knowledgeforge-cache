This paper presents experimental measurements and theoretical analysis of the radar cross section (RCS) of polyfoam towers, modeled as dielectric columns. Measurements were conducted at 35 and 70 GHz over a range of tower diameters (2.5 to 8 wavelengths) and dielectric constants (1.01 to 1.2), showing good agreement with theoretical predictions based on Bessel function interference patterns.
