This review highlights the transformative role of click chemistry, particularly copper-catalyzed (CuAAC) and strain-promoted (SPAAC) azide–alkyne cycloadditions, in advancing cancer therapeutics. It details how these reactions enable the modular, efficient, and biocompatible assembly of multifunctional agents for immunotherapy, antibody–drug conjugates, PROTACs, exosome modification, and photodynamic therapy.
