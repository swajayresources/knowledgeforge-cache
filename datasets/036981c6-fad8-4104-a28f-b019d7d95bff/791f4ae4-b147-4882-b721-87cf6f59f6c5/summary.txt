This paper reviews the pivotal role of click chemistry, particularly copper(I)-catalyzed azide–alkyne cycloaddition (CuAAC) and strain-promoted azide–alkyne cycloaddition (SPAAC), in advancing cancer therapeutics. It highlights applications including cancer immunotherapy, antibody–drug conjugates (ADCs), proteolysis-targeting chimeras (PROTACs), targeted combination therapies, exosome modification, and photodynamic therapy.
