The paper investigates electromagnetic (EM) emissions preceding fracture in heterogeneous media, applying fractal statistical analysis under the critical point (CP) hypothesis. It identifies key fractal features signaling approach to failure, such as increased spatial correlation, emergence of memory effects, loss of multifractality, and divergence of energy release rate, consistent with percolation theory of fracture.
