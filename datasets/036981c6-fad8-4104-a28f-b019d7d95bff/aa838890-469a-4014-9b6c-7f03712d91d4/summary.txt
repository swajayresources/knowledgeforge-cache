This paper reports the first experimental realization of birefringent transformation optics (TO) devices that perform different functions for orthogonal polarization states of light. The devices are based on lithographically defined metal/dielectric waveguides, where adiabatic variations of the waveguide shape enable control of the effective refractive indices for TE and TM modes, allowing the creation of Luneburg lenses and cloaking devices with polarization-dependent behavior.
