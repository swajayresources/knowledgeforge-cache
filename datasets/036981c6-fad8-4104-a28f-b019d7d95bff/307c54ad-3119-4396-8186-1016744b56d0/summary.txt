This paper investigates the radiation dynamics of an electric dipole source placed near or inside a discrete electromagnetic invisibility cloak. It reveals that the discrete geometry of the cloak causes local-field effects and coupling to leaky modes, which can both inhibit and unexpectedly enhance the emission of the source, deviating from the ideal continuous cloak behavior.
