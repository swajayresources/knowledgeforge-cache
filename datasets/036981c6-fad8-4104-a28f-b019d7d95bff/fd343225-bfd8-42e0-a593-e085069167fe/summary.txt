This paper presents a novel electron beam (e-beam) cross-linking method to enhance the electrical conductivity of carbon nanotube (CNT) thin films without compromising optical transparency. Nanopatterning of CNT films improves optical transparency and thermopower (Seebeck coefficient), while CNT/polymer nanocomposites using poly(N-isopropylacrylamide) (PNIPAM) demonstrate high electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding efficiency and thermal signature reduction with maintained transparency.
