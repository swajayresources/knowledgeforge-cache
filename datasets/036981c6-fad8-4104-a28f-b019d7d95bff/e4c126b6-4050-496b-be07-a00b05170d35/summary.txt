This paper presents the design and experimental validation of an adaptive radar absorbing structure using a PIN diode controlled active frequency selective surface (FSS). The absorber replaces the conventional resistive layer of a Salisbury screen with an active FSS whose impedance can be dynamically tuned by controlling the bias current of embedded PIN diodes, enabling reflectivity control over the 9 to 13 GHz frequency band.
