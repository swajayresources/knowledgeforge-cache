This paper presents an alternative approach to designing cloaks and radar cross section (RCS)-reducing absorbers for radar targets using transformation electromagnetics (TE), differing from classical transformation optics (TO) methods. The proposed method uses realistic, wideband materials that are less sensitive to polarization and incident angle, overcoming key limitations of TO-based cloaks such as narrow bandwidth, anisotropy, and impractical material parameters.
