This paper presents the design and development of a novel flexible hybrid nanocomposite (FHN) structure combining a frequency selective surface (FSS) printed on a flexible polyimide film backed by aluminum foil integrated with a customized nanocomposite sheet made of graphite nanopowder and zinc oxide fillers in a polyurethane matrix. The FHN structure achieves wideband radar cross section (RCS) reduction across the entire X-band (8–12 GHz) with a minimum reflection coefficient of −28 dB at 10.2
