This paper presents the design and fabrication of broad band radar absorbing composite laminates based on a 2-layer Dällenbach type absorber using E-glass fabric/epoxy composites with carbon nanotube (CNT) fillers. The electromagnetic properties were optimized by adjusting the number of plies and CNT content to achieve optimal absorption around 10 GHz, resulting in a fabricated laminate with a -10 dB absorption bandwidth of 7.4 GHz.
