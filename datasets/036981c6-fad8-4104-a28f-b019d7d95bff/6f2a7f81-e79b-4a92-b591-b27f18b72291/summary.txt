This paper presents a Pancharatnam–Berry (PB) phase-based coding metasurface for terahertz wave radar cross section (RCS) reduction. The metasurface uses train-symbol-shaped meta-particles with varying orientations to achieve 1-, 2-, and 3-bit coding, enabling wideband RCS reduction and flexible wave manipulation. The design achieves approximately -13.5 dB RCS reduction in the 0.85–1.6 THz band and is polarization-insensitive.
