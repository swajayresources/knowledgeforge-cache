This paper analyzes the impact of the recent CMD-3 measurement of the e+e- -> pi+pi- cross section on the Standard Model prediction of the muon anomalous magnetic moment (g-2). The authors perform a joint fit of the world data on the e+e- -> hadrons cross sections, scaling the experimental uncertainties to account for incompatibilities between experiments, and obtain an updated estimate of the leading-order hadronic contribution to g-2.
