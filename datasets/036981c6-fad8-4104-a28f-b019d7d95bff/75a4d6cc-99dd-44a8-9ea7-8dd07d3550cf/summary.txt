This paper analyzes the impact of the recent CMD-3 measurement of the e+e- -> pi+pi- cross section on the Standard Model prediction of the muon anomalous magnetic moment. The authors perform a joint fit of the world data on the e+e- -> hadrons channels, scaling the experimental uncertainties to account for incompatibilities between experiments, and obtain a leading-order hadronic contribution consistent with previous results despite the inclusion of the new CMD-3 data.
