This study investigates the radar absorbing properties of polymer composites reinforced with multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNTs) of different particle sizes (8 nm, 30–50 nm, and 48–78 nm). The composites were prepared with 1% CNT loading in polyester resin and characterized using XRD, SEM, VSM, and network analysis in the 8–12 GHz frequency range.
