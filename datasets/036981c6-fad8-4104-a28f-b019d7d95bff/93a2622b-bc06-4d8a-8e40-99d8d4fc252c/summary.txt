This 1994 dissertation by A. Chatterjee, J.L. Volakis, and J. Nguyen develops and analyzes finite element methods (FEM) for electromagnetic wave propagation and scattering, focusing on three-dimensional unbounded domain problems. It emphasizes the use of absorbing boundary conditions (ABCs) to truncate the computational domain efficiently, the formulation of vector finite elements to avoid spurious solutions, and the optimization and parallelization of the FEM code for large-scale simulations.
