This paper analyzes the concept of using stealth Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) to support the air defense system of Indonesia's new capital city in Kalimantan. It emphasizes the need for a smart defense system combining hard (technological) and soft (local wisdom) defense, highlighting stealth UAVs' advantages in intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, and attack missions with minimized detection risk.
