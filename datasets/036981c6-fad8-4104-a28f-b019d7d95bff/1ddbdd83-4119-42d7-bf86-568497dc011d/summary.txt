This paper presents an initial methodology to estimate and optimize the recurring labor costs in manufacturing aircraft composite structures using liquid moulding technologies, specifically Resin Transfer Moulding (RTM) and Vacuum-Assisted Resin Transfer Moulding (VARTM). By applying cost equations to detailed process steps, the study compares the cost-effectiveness of RTM and VARTM for producing an integral stiffened aircraft flap, finding that VARTM incurs approximately 10% higher labor hours
