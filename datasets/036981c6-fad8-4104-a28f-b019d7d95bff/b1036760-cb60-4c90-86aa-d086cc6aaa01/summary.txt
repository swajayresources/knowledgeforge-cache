This paper proposes an ultra-wideband radar cross section reduction (RCSR) metasurface using non-resonant miniaturized artificial magnetic conductor (MAMC) unit cells arranged in a chessboard pattern. The unit cells, composed of stacked non-resonant patches separated by thin dielectric substrates, are optimized via a genetic algorithm to achieve a wide 10-dB RCSR bandwidth exceeding 140% fractional bandwidth. A low-cost metasurface using FR-4 substrate was fabricated and experimentally validated
