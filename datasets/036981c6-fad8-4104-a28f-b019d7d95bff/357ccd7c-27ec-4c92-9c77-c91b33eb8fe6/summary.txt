This paper presents the first experimental demonstration of an active electromagnetic 3D interior cloak capable of hiding a conductive cylindrical target in free space at 1.2 GHz. The cloak consists of a conformal array of radiating sources that generate a field discontinuity to cancel the scattered electromagnetic fields from the target.
