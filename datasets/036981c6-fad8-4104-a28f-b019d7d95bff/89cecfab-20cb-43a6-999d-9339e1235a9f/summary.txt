This paper proposes a broadband linear polarization conversion metasurface (MS) based on a metal cut-wire structure to achieve radar cross section (RCS) reduction. By designing coding metasurfaces with elements having a 180° cross-polarization phase difference, the authors demonstrate broadband RCS reduction of over 10 dB in the 6–14 GHz range, confirmed by both simulations and experimental measurements.
