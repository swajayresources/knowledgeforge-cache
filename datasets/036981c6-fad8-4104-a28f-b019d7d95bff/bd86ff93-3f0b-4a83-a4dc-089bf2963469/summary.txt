This paper introduces a novel canonical reduction method for the motion of three point vortices in a two-dimensional ideal fluid, employing a two-stage process: a change to Jacobi coordinates followed by a Nambu reduction. The resulting coordinates reveal that the vortex dynamics evolve on a two-dimensional manifold whose topology depends on the sign of a parameter κ₂, yielding either a spherical phase space (κ₂ > 0) or a hyperboloid (κ₂ < 0).
