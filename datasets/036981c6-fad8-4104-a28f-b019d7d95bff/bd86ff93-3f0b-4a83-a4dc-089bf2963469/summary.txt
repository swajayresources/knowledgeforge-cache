This paper introduces a novel canonical reduction of the three-point vortex problem in a two-dimensional ideal fluid, achieved through a two-stage process involving Jacobi coordinates followed by a Nambu reduction. The resulting reduced system evolves on a two-dimensional manifold whose topology depends on the sign of a parameter κ₂, being spherical for κ₂ > 0 and hyperboloidal for κ₂ < 0.
