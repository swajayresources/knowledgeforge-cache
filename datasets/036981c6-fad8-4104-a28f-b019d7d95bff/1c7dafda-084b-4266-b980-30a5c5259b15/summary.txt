This paper investigates the preparation of natural ferrite powders derived from corrosion products (rust) of ferrous metals collected from Surabaya, Malang, and Bangkalan, Indonesia, and evaluates their microwave absorbing properties in the X-band frequency range (8-12 GHz). Characterization by XRF, XRD, and VSM confirmed the presence of magnetite (Fe3O4) phase with soft magnetic properties.
