This paper analyzes and compares the kinematic characteristics of stealth coronal mass ejections (CMEs) in three-dimensional space for the period 2008–2014. It distinguishes three CME groups—stealth CMEs, front-side CMEs associated with X-ray flares and filament eruptions, and all CMEs—and finds that stealth CMEs generally have lower velocity, kinetic energy, mass, and angular size.
