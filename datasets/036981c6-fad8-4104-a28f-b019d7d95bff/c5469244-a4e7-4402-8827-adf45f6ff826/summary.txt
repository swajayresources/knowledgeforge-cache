This paper presents a detailed study on the relative dose-to-water (D_W) response of alanine dosimeters for high-energy electron beams using a Fricke primary standard at the Swiss Federal Office of Metrology (METAS). The work includes the calibration of the Fricke solution via total absorption of monoenergetic electron beams, irradiation of alanine detectors, and comparison with Monte Carlo simulations and other primary standards.
