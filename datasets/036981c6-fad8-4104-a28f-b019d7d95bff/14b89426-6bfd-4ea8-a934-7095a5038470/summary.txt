This paper presents the design and simulation of a high gain microstrip antenna based on a Photonic BandGap (PBG) structure covered with Split Ring Resonators (SRR) to achieve low Radar Cross Section (RCS) without degrading antenna performance. The PBG structure improves antenna gain by inhibiting surface waves, while the SRR cover reduces the RCS by manipulating electromagnetic wave scattering.
