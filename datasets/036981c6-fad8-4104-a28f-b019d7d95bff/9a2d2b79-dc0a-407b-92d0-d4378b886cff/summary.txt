This paper analyzes the performance of chipless RFID tags by focusing on Radar Cross Section (RCS) and Bit Error Rate (BER). It demonstrates that the arrangement of resonators significantly impacts the tag's RCS and BER, with periodic configurations providing higher RCS and lower BER compared to non-periodic arrangements. The study uses both theoretical and experimental methods to evaluate different tag topologies and emphasizes that BER is a more meaningful figure of merit than bit/cm² or bit/H
