This paper presents a novel electron beam (e-beam) cross-linking method to enhance the electrical conductivity of carbon nanotube (CNT) thin films without compromising optical transparency. Nanopatterning of CNT films improves optical transparency and thermopower (Seebeck coefficient), while multifunctional CNT/poly(N-isopropylacrylamide) (PNIPAM) composites demonstrate effective electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding and thermal signature reduction with maintained transparency.
