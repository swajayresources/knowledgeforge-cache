This paper presents X-band radar cross section (RCS) measurements of insects as a function of the angle between the insect body axis and the plane of polarization. It finds that for larger insects, the maximum RCS occurs when the electric field vector (E-vector) is perpendicular to the body axis, and it also reports new measurements for small insects like aphids and planthoppers. The study compiles a comprehensive summary of insect RCS data at X-band, highlighting the importance of polarization
