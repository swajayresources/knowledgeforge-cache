This paper investigates the radar cross section (RCS) of a perfect electromagnetic conductor (PEMC) strip embedded in various lossy soil media. Using the duality transformation introduced by Lindell and Sihvola, the authors develop an analytic theory to evaluate the RCS of the PEMC strip, comparing it with the limiting cases of perfect electric conductor (PEC) and perfect magnetic conductor (PMC).
