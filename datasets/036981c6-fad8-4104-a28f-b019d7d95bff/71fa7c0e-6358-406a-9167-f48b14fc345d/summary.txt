This paper reports the synthesis of barium hexaferrite (BaFe12O19) nanoparticles via a sol-gel auto combustion method optimized for radar absorber material (RAM) applications. The study investigates the effects of pH and stirring time on the microstructure, phase formation, and magnetic properties, finding that pH 7.5 and stirring times of 1-2 hours yield the best magnetic performance with coercivity up to 0.6 Tesla and saturation magnetization up to 55.54 emu/g.
