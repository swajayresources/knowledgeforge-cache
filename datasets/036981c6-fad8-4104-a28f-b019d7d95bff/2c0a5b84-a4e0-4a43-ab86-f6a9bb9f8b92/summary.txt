This paper reviews research progress on nanostructured radar absorbing materials (RAMs), which offer enhanced microwave absorption compared to bulk counterparts due to increased surface area and interface polarization. The authors examine four main types of nanostructured RAMs: nanocrystal RAMs, core-shell nanocomposite RAMs, MWCNT-inorganic material composites, and nanostructured carbon-polymer composites, detailing their microwave properties and performance characteristics.
