This 1989 research paper investigates whether incorporating significant wave height (SWH) and radar cross section (RCS) data can improve the accuracy of radar altimeter wind speed measurements over oceans. Using multiple linear regression on 236 buoy-altimeter data pairs, the authors find marginal but statistically significant improvements in wind speed retrievals when including RCS and SWH, especially with the smoothed-Brown (SB) algorithm.
