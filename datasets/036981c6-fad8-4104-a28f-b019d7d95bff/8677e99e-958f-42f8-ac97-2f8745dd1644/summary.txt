This paper explores the critical role of Radar Cross-Section (RCS) in the design of advanced stealth aircraft, emphasizing how minimizing RCS enhances survivability in modern aerial combat. It reviews the definition of RCS, its measurement, and three primary calculation methods—Geometric Optics, Physical Optics, and the Method of Moments—detailing their applications and limitations.
