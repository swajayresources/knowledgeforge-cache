This paper presents two types of low-frequency broadband composite metamaterial absorbers, MA1 and MA2, designed for ultra-high-frequency (UHF) band radar applications. The absorbers are made of dendritic metamaterial units with different physical sizes and structures, which absorb different microwave frequencies. The results show that both absorbers achieve high absorption rates (>90%) in the frequency range of 0.3-1.1 GHz.
