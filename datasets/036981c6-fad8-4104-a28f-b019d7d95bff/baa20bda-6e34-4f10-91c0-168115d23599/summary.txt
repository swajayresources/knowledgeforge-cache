This paper investigates the microwave absorption properties of radar absorbing materials (RAM) composed of MnZn ferrite particles embedded in a silicone rubber matrix, focusing on how varying ferrite volume fractions affect complex magnetic permeability and dielectric permittivity in the 2 to 18 GHz frequency range. The study uses transmission/reflection measurements and impedance matching theory to analyze reflection loss, revealing that increased ferrite content generally enhances permeability
