This paper presents a novel multilayer sandwich fabric-based composite material designed for infrared stealth and superior thermal insulation. The composite integrates a carbon nanotube-doped aerogel (CNTAs) layer on polyimide (PI) fabric with a low emissivity Al-doped ZnO (ZAO) coating, achieving low surface emissivity (<0.5) and ultra-low thermal conductivity (~0.013 W/(m·K)).
