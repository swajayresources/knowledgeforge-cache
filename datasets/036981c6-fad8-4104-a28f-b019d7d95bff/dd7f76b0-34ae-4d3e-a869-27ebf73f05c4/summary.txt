This paper presents a multilevel preconditioning method for p-adaptive finite element analysis of electromagnetic scattering problems. The approach combines a p-type multiplicative Schwarz (pMUS) method with auxiliary space preconditioning (ASP) and algebraic multigrid (AMG) techniques to efficiently solve large, sparse matrix systems arising from hierarchical high-order finite elements.
