This paper investigates the effect of various reduction methods on graphene oxide (GO) to produce reduced graphene oxide (rGO) and evaluates their electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding performance in the X-band frequency range (8.2–12.4 GHz). The study compares single-step reductions (hydrazine hydrate and thermal annealing) and novel double-step reductions involving hydrazine hydrate followed by low energy ion beam implantation or swift heavy ion irradiation.
