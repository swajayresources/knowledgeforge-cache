This paper investigates the detection of barely visible impact damage (BVID) in aircraft composite structures using nonlinear elastic wave spectroscopy (NEWS). Two nonlinear ultrasonic methods, single-mode nonlinear resonance ultrasound spectroscopy (SIMONRAS) and nonlinear wave modulation spectroscopy (NWMS), were applied to carbon fiber composite plates with unknown mechanical properties and damage sizes.
