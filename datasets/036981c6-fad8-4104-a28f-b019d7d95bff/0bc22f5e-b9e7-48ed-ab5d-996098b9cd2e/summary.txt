This paper presents a novel active flow control strategy using deep reinforcement learning to achieve hydrodynamic stealth for a bluff body, such as a circular cylinder, in a uniform flow. The method uses a group of windward-suction-leeward-blowing (WSLB) actuators and an array of velocity sensors to mitigate the cylinder's hydrodynamic signatures, reducing the velocity deficit in the wake by 99.5% compared to the uncontrolled case.
