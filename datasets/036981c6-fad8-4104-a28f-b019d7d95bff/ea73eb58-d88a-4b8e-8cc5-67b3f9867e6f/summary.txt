This paper proposes and demonstrates a low-loss infrared invisibility cloak composed of identical nanosized chalcogenide glass resonators arranged in concentric cylindrical arrays. The cloak achieves the desired spatial distribution of effective permeability by varying air fractions between resonators rather than changing resonator dimensions, and its performance is verified through full-wave simulations of the multiresonator structure.
