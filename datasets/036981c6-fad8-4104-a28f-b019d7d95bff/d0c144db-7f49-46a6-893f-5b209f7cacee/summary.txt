This paper presents a tunable broadband radar absorbing screen combining graphene sheets with Hilbert curve metal strip arrays for radar cross-section (RCS) reduction in the microwave band. The device achieves a relative bandwidth of 68.1% (8.9-18.1 GHz) at 0 eV chemical potential with electrical tunability through external voltage control, enabling central frequency tuning from 13.5 GHz to 19.0 GHz.
