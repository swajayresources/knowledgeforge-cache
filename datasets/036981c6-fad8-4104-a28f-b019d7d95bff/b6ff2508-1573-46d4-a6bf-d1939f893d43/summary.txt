This paper presents a novel polarization conversion metasurface (PCM) based on a double-heads arrow unit cell and its rotated variants, designed for ultra-wideband radar cross section (RCS) reduction. The structure achieves more than 10 dB RCS reduction from 9 to 40 GHz (126.5% bandwidth) for both TM- and TE-polarized waves, with excellent agreement between simulation and measurement results.
