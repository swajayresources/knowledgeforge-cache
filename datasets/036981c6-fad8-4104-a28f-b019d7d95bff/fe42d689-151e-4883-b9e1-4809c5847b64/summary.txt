This paper presents a novel backfire helical feed for a paraboloidal reflector, designed to meet specific antenna requirements. The feed consists of a 3.5-turn helix wrapped around a 1/4-in diameter center conductor, supported by a bead in rigid coax. The design meets the requirements of a 20 percent bandwidth at L-band, right-hand circular polarization, and a peak power capability of 200 kW unpressurized.
