This paper reviews the finite-difference time-domain (FD-TD) method for numerical modeling of electromagnetic wave scattering and radar cross section (RCS). It covers theoretical foundations, algorithmic details, and validations against canonical two- and three-dimensional targets, demonstrating FD-TD's accuracy, robustness, and potential for modeling complex, electrically large structures.
