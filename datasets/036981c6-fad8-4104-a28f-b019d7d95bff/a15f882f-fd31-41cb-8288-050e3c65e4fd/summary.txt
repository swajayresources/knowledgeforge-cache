This research investigates the fabrication, characterization, and sound absorption performance of a composite made from water hyacinth biochar (WHBC) and polyvinyl alcohol (PVA). The WH biochar was produced by pyrolysis at different temperatures (400, 500, 600°C) and blended with PVA to form disk-shaped composites.
