The paper addresses the inverse problem of reconstructing the shape of a perfect conductor from the electric far-field pattern of time-harmonic electromagnetic waves scattered by it. It presents and analyzes two optimization-based methods—the Kirsch-Kress method and the Colton-Monk method—both designed to handle the ill-posed, nonlinear nature of this inverse scattering problem.
