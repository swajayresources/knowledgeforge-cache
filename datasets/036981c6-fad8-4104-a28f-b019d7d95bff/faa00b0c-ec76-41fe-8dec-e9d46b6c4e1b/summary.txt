This paper presents a checkerboard plasma electromagnetic surface (PES) designed to reduce bistatic radar cross section (RCS) of metal plates through phase cancellation without using absorptive materials. By combining plasma frequency selective surfaces (FSS) with metallic FSS in a checkerboard pattern, the authors achieve over 140° reflection phase difference across a wide frequency band (3-14 GHz) and various incident angles, resulting in significant RCS reduction for both monostatic and bista
