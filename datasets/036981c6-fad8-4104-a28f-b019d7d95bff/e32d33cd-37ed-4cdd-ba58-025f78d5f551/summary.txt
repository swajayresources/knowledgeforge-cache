This paper experimentally demonstrates that bilayer composite magnetic systems, combining random anisotropy hexaferrite-based nanocomposites and soft metallic magnetic materials in submillimetric layers, significantly enhance radar absorption performance. The bilayer structures show up to 40% increase in reflection loss (up to 35 dB) and a 20% increase in absorption bandwidth under real far-field radar conditions, with absorption frequency tunable by layer thickness and composition.
