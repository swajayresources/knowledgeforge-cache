This paper presents the development of polyurethane foam-based sandwich structures designed for radar absorption and load-bearing capability. The structures combine glass fiber epoxy face skins with carbon black, carbon fiber epoxy back skins, and polyurethane foam cores reinforced with graphite powder or carbonyl iron. Optimal configurations achieved up to -31.85 dB attenuation and nearly zero radar signature at multiple frequencies in the X-band and Ku-band.
