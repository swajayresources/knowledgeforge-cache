This paper evaluates the Radar Cross Section (RCS) measurement of a metallic target inside a Reverberation Chamber (RC) by extracting the ballistic wave between antenna and target amid the diffuse field. The method shows good agreement with classical Anechoic Chamber (AC) measurements but highlights that accuracy strongly depends on maintaining the same stirrer position during measurements with and without the target.
