This paper investigates a composite electromagnetic wave absorber made of both sendust (Al 5%, Si 10%, Fe 85%) and aluminum particles dispersed in polystyrene resin, aiming to achieve good absorption properties above 1 GHz. By varying the aluminum particle size and the volume mixture ratio of sendust and aluminum, the frequency dependence of the relative complex permeability (µ*) can be controlled, enabling flexible absorber design.
