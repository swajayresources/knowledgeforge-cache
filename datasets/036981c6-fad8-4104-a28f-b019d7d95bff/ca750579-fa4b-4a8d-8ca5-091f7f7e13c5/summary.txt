This paper presents a novel model-order reduction (MOR) technique, called the Enhanced Reduced Basis Method (EnRBM), applied to hybrid electromagnetic analysis methods involving the Generalized Impedance Matrix (GIM). The approach significantly reduces computational time for frequency-domain scattering problems in both open and closed domains, achieving up to an order of magnitude speed-up while maintaining high accuracy.
