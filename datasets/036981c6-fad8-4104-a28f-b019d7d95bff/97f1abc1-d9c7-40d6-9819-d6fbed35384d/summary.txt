This paper analyzes the recipient anonymity and unlinkability guarantees of the Umbra stealth address scheme on Ethereum and its Layer-2 networks such as Polygon, Arbitrum, and Optimism. It identifies four heuristics based on user behavior that significantly reduce or nullify Umbra's privacy guarantees, linking a substantial portion of stealth payments to their real recipients.
