This paper demonstrates that a single cylindrical cloak can effectively mold acoustic, electromagnetic, and water waves. The cloak works through artificial anisotropy and exhibits broadband cloaking capabilities, with experimental and numerical results confirming reduced scattering and field attenuation inside the cloak for frequencies ranging from 2.6 to 7 GHz for microwaves, 3 to 8 kHz for sound waves, and 5 to 16 Hz for surface liquid waves.
