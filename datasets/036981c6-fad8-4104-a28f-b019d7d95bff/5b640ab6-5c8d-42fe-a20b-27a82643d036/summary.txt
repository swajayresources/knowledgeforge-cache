This paper proposes the Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) as a numerical approach for electromagnetic wave scattering, validating it across Rayleigh, Mie, and geometric optics regimes for canonical and non-canonical geometries. The method is benchmarked against analytical Mie theory for cylinders and spheres, and against the Discretized Mie-Formalism for hexagonal cylinders, showing excellent agreement.
