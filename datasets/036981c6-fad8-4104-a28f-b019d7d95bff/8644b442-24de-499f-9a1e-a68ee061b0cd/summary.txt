This paper presents an optimization approach for selecting Frequency Selective Surfaces (FSS) as radar absorbing materials (RAM) using Genetic Algorithm to maximize absorption in the 8-12 GHz frequency range. The study analyzes various FSS configurations combined with eight different ferrite materials, demonstrating that triple square FSS achieves maximum absorption of 9.19 dB at 10 GHz, significantly outperforming single ferrite coatings alone.
