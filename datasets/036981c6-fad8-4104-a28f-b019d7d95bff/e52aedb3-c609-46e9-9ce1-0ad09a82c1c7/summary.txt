This paper presents a novel planar wideband radar cross-section (RCS) reduction technique using a chessboard structure composed of two different artificial magnetic conductor (AMC) unit cell arrays: saltire arrow and four E-shaped patches. The design achieves a wide operational bandwidth of 9.4–23.28 GHz (85%) with more than 10 dB RCS reduction by exploiting a 180° ± 30° reflection phase difference between the AMC tiles, validated by both simulations and measurements.
