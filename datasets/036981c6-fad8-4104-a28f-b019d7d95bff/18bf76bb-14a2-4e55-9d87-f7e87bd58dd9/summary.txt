This paper reports a measurement of the differential cross section for muon antineutrino charged-current interactions producing two or more neutrons with low visible hadronic energy on hydrocarbon at an average antineutrino energy of 6 GeV using the MINERvA detector. The results show discrepancies with predictions from several leading neutrino interaction models, highlighting deficiencies in neutron production and final state interaction modeling.
