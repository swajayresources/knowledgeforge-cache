This paper describes how systems biology approaches, including computational modeling, were used to guide the discovery and development of the anti-ErbB3 antibody seribantumab (MM-121) for cancer treatment. The authors highlight how their systems-level understanding of the ErbB signaling network identified ErbB3 as a critical target, and how modeling and preclinical studies informed the design, dosing, and biomarker strategies for clinical testing of seribantumab.
