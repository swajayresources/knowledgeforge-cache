This paper presents a 3D visible-light invisibility cloak that can hide a macroscopic object from plain sight using a polyhedral transformation and isotropic materials. The cloak is designed to work in three orthogonal directions in 3D space and can guide light around a hidden object, making it return to its original path when incident from certain 3D perpendicular directions. The cloak is demonstrated experimentally using a cubic shape and a steel ball as the object to be hidden.
