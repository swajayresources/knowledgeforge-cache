This 1985 study investigates continuous filament wound grid-stiffened composite concepts for aircraft fuselage structures, focusing on three candidate grid designs: isogrid, orthogrid, and generalized orthogrid. Using Donnell-type stability equations including transverse shear deformation, the paper analyzes buckling behavior under uniaxial compression and combined loading, demonstrating that weight savings of 20-30% compared to metal designs are feasible with competitive structural performance,
