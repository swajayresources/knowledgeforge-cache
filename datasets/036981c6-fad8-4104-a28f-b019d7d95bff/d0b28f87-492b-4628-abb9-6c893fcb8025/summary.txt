This paper proposes a novel electromagnetic (EM) GL double layered cloak composed of two spherical annular layers with distinct metamaterials: an outer layer providing invisibility and an inner layer offering full absorption of EM waves from internal sources. The cloak ensures that external EM waves propagate undisturbed outside the cloak and cannot penetrate inside, while EM waves generated inside the concealed region are completely absorbed by the inner layer, preventing detection.
