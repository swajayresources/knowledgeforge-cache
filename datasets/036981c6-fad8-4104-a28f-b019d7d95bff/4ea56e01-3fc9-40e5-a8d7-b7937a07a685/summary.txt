This paper presents a novel ultra-broadband radar cross section (RCS) reduction technique for waveguide slot antennas by loading a three-layer metamaterial based on orthogonal double split-ring resonators on the antenna's weak current areas. Both simulated and experimental results demonstrate significant RCS reduction from approximately 4.7 GHz to 18 GHz along with gain enhancement and beamwidth narrowing, achieved without degrading the antenna's radiation performance.
