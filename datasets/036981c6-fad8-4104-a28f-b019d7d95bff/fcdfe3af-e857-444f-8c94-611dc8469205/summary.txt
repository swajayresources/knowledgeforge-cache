This paper proposes a fast computational method for broadband electromagnetic scattering analysis using an isogeometric boundary element method (IGABEM) based on non-uniform rational B-splines (NURBS). To accelerate multi-frequency calculations, it employs a series expansion technique for frequency decoupling and applies the second-order Arnoldi (SOAR) method for model order reduction, enabling efficient handling of large-scale problems.
