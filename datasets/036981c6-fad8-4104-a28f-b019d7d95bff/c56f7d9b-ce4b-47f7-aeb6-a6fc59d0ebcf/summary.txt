This paper presents a novel hybrid invisibility cloak that integrates transparent metasurfaces with zero-index materials (ZIMs) to achieve cloaking in transmission geometry. The design combines wavefront tailoring by metasurfaces and wave tunneling by ZIMs, enabling cloaking for arbitrary shapes with significantly reduced thickness and complexity.
