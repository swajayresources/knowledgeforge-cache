This paper proposes a novel low-profile multi-layered invisibility carpet cloak operating at visible wavelengths by using alternating layers of silicon and a Low Index Material (LIM) with relative dielectric constant less than 1. The LIM is realized using silver-coated CdSe/CdS quantum dot core–shell nanoparticles dispersed in a polymer host, which compensates silver loss and achieves negligible absorption.
