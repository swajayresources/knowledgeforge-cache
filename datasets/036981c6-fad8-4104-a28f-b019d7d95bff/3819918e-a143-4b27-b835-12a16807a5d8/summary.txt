This paper proposes a dual-band metamaterial perfect absorber for infrared stealth technology, which can reduce the scattering of IR laser light by more than 90% and suppress mid-wavelength IR and long-wavelength IR signatures by more than 92%. The absorber has a metal-insulator-metal structure and is designed to have surface plasmon polariton and magnetic polariton resonance peaks at 1.54 μm and 6.2 μm, respectively.
