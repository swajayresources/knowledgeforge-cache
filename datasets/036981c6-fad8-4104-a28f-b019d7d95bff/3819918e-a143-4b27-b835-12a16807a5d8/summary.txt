This paper proposes a dual-band metamaterial perfect absorber based on a metal–insulator–metal (MIM) structure designed for infrared (IR) stealth technology. It features two resonance peaks at 1.54 μm and 6.2 μm, targeting suppression of laser-guided missile signals and atmospheric IR absorption bands, respectively, achieving over 90% reduction in IR signatures across a wide temperature range and incident angles.
