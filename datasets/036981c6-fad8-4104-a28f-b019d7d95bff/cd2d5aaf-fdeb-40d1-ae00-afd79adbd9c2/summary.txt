This paper describes the preparation and microwave absorbing properties of a carbon fiber/polyurethane radar absorbing coating (CFPURAC). The coating is prepared with carbon fiber as the filler and polyurethane as the matrix, and multi-layer CFPURAC is prepared with polyurethane coating as the wave-transmitting material.
