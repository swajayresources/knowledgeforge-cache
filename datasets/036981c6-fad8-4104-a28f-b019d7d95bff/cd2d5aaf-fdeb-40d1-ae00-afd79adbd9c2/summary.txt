This paper investigates the preparation and microwave absorbing properties of carbon fiber/polyurethane radar absorbing coatings (CFPURAC). The study demonstrates that the absorbing performance is significantly influenced by the length and content of carbon fibers, as well as the matching order and thickness of multi-layer structures. The optimal configuration achieves an effective absorbing bandwidth of up to 13.1 GHz, covering the frequency range of 4.9 GHz to 18 GHz.
