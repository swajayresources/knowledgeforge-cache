This paper presents a novel approach to designing a carpet cloak that can operate for a wide range of incident angles using a deep neural network and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithm. The proposed method uses a single-layer passive metasurface consisting of 15 unit cells, each designed using the PSO algorithm in combination with a deep neural network. The results show that the designed carpet cloak operates well for all incident angles in the range of 0 to 65 degrees.
