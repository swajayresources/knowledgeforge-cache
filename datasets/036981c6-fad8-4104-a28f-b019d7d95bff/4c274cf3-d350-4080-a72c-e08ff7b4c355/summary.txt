This paper presents the development of an indoor X-band tracking radar system to measure the dynamic radar cross-section (RCS) of a mini unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) in flight, specifically a quadcopter. It demonstrates that the dynamic RCS, influenced by UAV motion such as propeller rotation, is significantly higher than static RCS measurements, leading to a 15% increase in the 50% probability of detection (PoD) range.
