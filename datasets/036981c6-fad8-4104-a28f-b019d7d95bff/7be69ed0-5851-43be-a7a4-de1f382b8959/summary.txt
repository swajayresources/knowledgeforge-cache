This paper presents the fabrication and evaluation of radar absorbing structures (RAS) made from hybrid composite materials shaped into three-dimensional 'C' and 'U' shells. The hybrid composites combine glass fiber/epoxy/carbon-black absorbing layers with carbon fiber reinforced epoxy reflection layers, achieving an average radar absorption efficiency of about 11 dB at 10 GHz.
