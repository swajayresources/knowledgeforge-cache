This paper proposes a novel electromagnetic stealth (ES) strategy that integrates an intelligent reflecting surface (IRS) with traditional electromagnetic wave absorbing materials (EWAMs) to reduce radar detection probability. By optimizing IRS reflection coefficients to destructively combine with residual reflections from EWAMs, the system minimizes the received signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) at the radar, achieving superior stealth performance even under dynamic radar-target scenarios.
