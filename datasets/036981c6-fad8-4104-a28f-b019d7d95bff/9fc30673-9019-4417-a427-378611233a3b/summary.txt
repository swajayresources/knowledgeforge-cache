This paper presents a novel graphene-based tunable broadband radar absorbing screen designed for radar cross section (RCS) reduction. The screen integrates a fractal Hilbert curve metal strip array with a chemical vapor deposition (CVD) graphene sheet, achieving wideband absorption from 8.9 GHz to 18.1 GHz and tunability of the central frequency from 13.5 GHz to 19.0 GHz by varying graphene's chemical potential.
