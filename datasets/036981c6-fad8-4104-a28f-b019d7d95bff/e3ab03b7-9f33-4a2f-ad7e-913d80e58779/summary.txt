This paper presents a novel graphene-based tunable broadband radar absorbing screen designed for radar cross section (RCS) reduction. The screen combines a fractal Hilbert curve metal strip array with a chemical vapor deposition (CVD) graphene sheet, enabling wideband absorption from 8.9 GHz to 18.1 GHz and tunability of the central frequency from 13.5 GHz to 19.0 GHz by varying the graphene's chemical potential.
