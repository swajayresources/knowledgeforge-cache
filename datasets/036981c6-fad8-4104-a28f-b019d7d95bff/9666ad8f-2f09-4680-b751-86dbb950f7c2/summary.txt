This paper presents a surface integral equation (SIE) formulation combined with the method of moments to evaluate the radar cross section (RCS) of arbitrarily shaped homogeneous or composite dielectric and conducting bodies of revolution (BOR). The approach reduces the 3D scattering problem to a 1D problem by exploiting rotational symmetry and Fourier decomposition, enabling efficient computation of surface currents and RCS for both axial and non-axial plane wave incidence.
