This paper extends the Kleinman-Martin integral equation method, originally developed for acoustic scattering, to the electromagnetic scattering problem by a dielectric body. It presents four families of single-boundary integral equations with parameters chosen to ensure unique solvability for all real frequencies, overcoming issues of spurious interior resonances. The paper rigorously analyzes well-posedness in energy spaces on both smooth and Lipschitz boundaries, using Helmholtz decomposition
