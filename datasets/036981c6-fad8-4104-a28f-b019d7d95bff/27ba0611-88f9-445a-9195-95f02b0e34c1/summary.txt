This paper presents a methodology for developing reduced-order macromodels of multiport passive electromagnetic devices that include embedded lumped elements. The approach uses finite-element discretization of Maxwell’s curl equations and incorporates lumped resistors, capacitors, and inductors directly into the state-space model.
