This paper investigates carbon foams prepared by a polymer sponge replication method as novel radar absorbing materials (RAMs) with broad absorbing bandwidth. It demonstrates that the electric conductivity of carbon foams, controlled by carbonization temperature, critically influences their microwave absorbing properties, with an optimal conductivity of 0.46 S/m yielding broad absorption over 4-15 GHz without impedance matching design.
