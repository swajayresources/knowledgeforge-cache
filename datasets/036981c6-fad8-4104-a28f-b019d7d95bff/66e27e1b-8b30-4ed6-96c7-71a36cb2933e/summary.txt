This paper presents the use of genetic algorithms (GAs) to optimize the design of wideband, multilayer Jaumann radar absorbing materials (RAM). The GA approach addresses the complex, multimodal optimization problem of selecting sheet resistances and spacer properties to maximize bandwidth and minimize reflectivity, including configurations with outer high-permittivity skins and active (frequency-agile) RAM.
