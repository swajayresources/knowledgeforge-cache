This paper proposes a novel design of microwave absorbers using multilayered composite materials with carbonyl iron powders (CIPs) to reduce radar cross section (RCS) over a broad frequency range of 2.0 to 18.0 GHz. The design employs transmission line theory (TLT) and particle swarm optimization (PSO) to optimize layer thicknesses and material compositions, resulting in thin absorbers with wide bandwidth and insensitivity to incident angle and polarization.
