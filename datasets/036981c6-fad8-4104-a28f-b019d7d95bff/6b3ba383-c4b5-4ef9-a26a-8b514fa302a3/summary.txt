This paper presents the development of active catheters and active guide wires that move like a snake using distributed shape memory alloy (SMA) actuators for minimally invasive diagnosis and therapy. It integrates communication and control IC chips to reduce lead wires, and incorporates ultra miniature fiber-optic pressure sensors, forward-looking ultrasonic probes, and magnetic position/orientation sensors near the catheter tip to enhance navigation and provide detailed real-time information.
