This 1992 paper by D.H. Middleton reviews the historical development and application of composite materials in aircraft structures, starting from early experiments in the 1940s through to advanced composites used in modern airliners like the Airbus A320. It covers material types, manufacturing techniques, structural advantages, and challenges in design and production, highlighting key innovations such as Redux bonding, boron and carbon fibers, and hybrid composites like Arall and Glare.
