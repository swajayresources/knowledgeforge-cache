This paper presents a large-scale study of the relationship between radar cross section (RCS) and optical magnitude for faint meteors, using simultaneous radar and optical observations. The authors confirm a strong linear correlation between RCS and V-band magnitude, derive a transformation function, and use it to estimate the luminosity function for sporadic meteors. The results show that the MU radar can detect interplanetary dust particles with masses ranging from 10⁻⁵ to 100 grams.
