This paper experimentally investigates electromagnetic coupling in split-ring resonator (SRR) metamaterial arrays using terahertz (THz) near-field microscopy. It identifies two distinct coupling regimes: near-field coupling via evanescent electric and magnetic fields in dense arrays, and far-field diffractive coupling at larger lattice periodicities matching resonance wavelengths.
