This paper presents the development and verification of a computational tool, RECOTA, for calculating the radar cross section (RCS) of complex targets using geometric approximation techniques. It combines physical optics, physical theory of diffraction, ray tracing, and semiempirical methods to model target geometry with facets and wedges, accounting for shadowing, multiple scattering, and discontinuities.
