This paper presents a hybrid Physical Optics (PO) - Method of Moments (MoM) - Ray Tracing (RT) algorithm for efficient Radar Cross Section (RCS) calculation of electrically large objects coated with Radar Absorbing Materials (RAM). The method evaluates the reflection coefficient of multilayer structures (metallic surfaces covered with RAM) using either a Periodic Method of Moments or a transmission line model, enabling accurate and fast RCS predictions compared to full-wave methods like MLFMM.
