This paper presents a method to reduce satellite electromagnetic scattering by covering satellites with carbon nanostructured multilayer radar absorbing materials (RAM). Using industrial-grade multiwalled carbon nanotubes (MWCNTs) dispersed in epoxy resin, the authors design and optimize multilayer absorbers via particle swarm optimization (PSO) to achieve reflection below -20 dB over 2-18 GHz, including oblique incidence.
