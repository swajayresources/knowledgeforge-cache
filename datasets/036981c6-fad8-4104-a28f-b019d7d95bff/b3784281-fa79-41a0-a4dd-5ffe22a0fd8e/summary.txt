This paper investigates the impact of rivet fasteners on the radar cross-section (RCS) performance of radar absorbing structures (RAS) used in aircraft air intake ducts. A radar absorbing material (RAM) with a thickness of 6.25 mm providing more than -10 dB reflection loss from 4 to 18 GHz was designed, and the effect of rivet configurations and rivet surface area on RCS degradation was studied through electromagnetic simulations.
