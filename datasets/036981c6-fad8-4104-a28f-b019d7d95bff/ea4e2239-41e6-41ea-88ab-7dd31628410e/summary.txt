This paper investigates the application of chiral layers and metamaterials as radar absorbing materials for reducing radar cross section (RCS). The authors analyze the reflection properties of a perfect electric conductor (PEC) plate coated with chiral layers under various conditions, including nondispersive, dispersive, and chiral nihility, and demonstrate the design of zero reflection coatings and microwave absorbers.
