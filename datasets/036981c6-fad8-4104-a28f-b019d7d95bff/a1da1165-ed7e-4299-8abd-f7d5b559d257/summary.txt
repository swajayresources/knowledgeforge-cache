This paper presents the design, manufacture, and application of a new standard reflecting plate for calibrating radar absorbing materials measurement systems in the 3 mm band (90-100 GHz). The plate features a truncated pyramid structure made of high permittivity metal, providing nominal reflectivities of -5 dB, -10 dB, and -15 dB with high stability and ease of use.
