This paper proposes a novel design of microwave absorbers based on multilayered composite materials containing carbonyl iron powders (CIPs) to reduce radar cross section (RCS) over a wide frequency band from 2.0 to 18.0 GHz. Using transmission line theory (TLT) and particle swarm optimization (PSO), the authors design and optimize multilayer absorbers with different layer configurations, achieving thin structures with wide bandwidth and insensitivity to incident wave angles and polarizations.
