This paper presents an optimal design methodology for broad-band radar absorbing composite laminates using a 2-layer Dällenbach absorber configuration. The researchers fabricated E-glass/epoxy composite laminates with carbon nanotube (CNT) dispersions and employed genetic algorithms to optimize layer thicknesses and CNT content for maximum absorption performance at 10 GHz, achieving a measured -10 dB absorbing bandwidth of 7.4 GHz in a 4.28 mm thick composite structure.
