This paper develops a radar cross section (RCS) constraint model for three-dimensional flight-path optimization to reduce radar detection time of aircraft. Using spline approximations of RCS and a simplified flight mechanics model, the study demonstrates that optimized trajectories can substantially decrease detection time compared to conventional flight paths.
