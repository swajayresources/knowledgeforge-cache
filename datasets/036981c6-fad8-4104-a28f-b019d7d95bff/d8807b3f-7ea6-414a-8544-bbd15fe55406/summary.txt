This paper presents hybrid numerical methods combining structured finite difference time domain (FDTD) with unstructured finite element time domain (FETD) or finite volume time domain (FVTD) schemes on overlapping grids for electromagnetic scattering simulations. The approach efficiently handles complex geometries by using structured meshes in free space and unstructured meshes near scatterers, enabling accurate and computationally efficient solutions.
