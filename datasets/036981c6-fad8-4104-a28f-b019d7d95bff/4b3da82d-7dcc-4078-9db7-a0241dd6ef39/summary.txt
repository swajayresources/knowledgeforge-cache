This paper proposes a novel intelligent reflecting surface (IRS)-aided electromagnetic stealth system mounted on targets to evade radar detection by dynamically optimizing IRS reflections to minimize the received radar signal power. It develops optimal and low-complexity IRS reflection design algorithms for both single-radar and multi-radar scenarios using Lagrange multiplier and MMSE methods, alongside practical angle-of-arrival (AoA) and path gain estimation schemes.
