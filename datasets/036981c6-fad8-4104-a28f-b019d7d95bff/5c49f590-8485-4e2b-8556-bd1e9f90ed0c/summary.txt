This paper proposes a novel framework for risk assessment of nonlinear cyber-physical systems (CPS) under stealth attacks, which are difficult to detect and can cause critical system failures. The framework introduces the concept of the Attacker’s Stealth Reachable (ASR) set to approximate the states an attacker can induce without detection, and constructs a risk field to formally describe scenario-specific risk distributions.
