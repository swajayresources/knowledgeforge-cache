This paper presents a dual-band metamaterial perfect absorber with a metal-insulator-metal (MIM) structure designed for infrared stealth technology. The absorber features surface plasmon polariton (SPP) and magnetic polariton (MP) resonance peaks at 1.54 μm and 6.2 μm respectively, enabling suppression of laser-guided missile detection and thermal imaging signatures.
