This paper presents detailed modeling and analysis of Radar Cross Section (RCS) for static simple and complex objects using MATLAB Radar Toolbox and POFACET GUI. It covers RCS variations with respect to aspect angle, size, and frequency for shapes such as spheres, cylinders, ellipsoids, flat plates, truncated cones, and combined complex objects, including dynamic RCS analysis using Swerling statistical models relevant for high-speed debris in Low Earth Orbit.
