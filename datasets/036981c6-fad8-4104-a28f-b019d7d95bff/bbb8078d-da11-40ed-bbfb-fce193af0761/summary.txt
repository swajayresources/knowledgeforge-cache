This paper reports the fabrication of highly effective electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding materials by coating cellulose papers with silver nanowires (AgNWs) using a facile dip-coating process. The resulting AgNW/cellulose papers exhibit a hierarchical structure with AgNWs predominantly on the surface, achieving high electrical conductivity (up to 67.51 S/cm) and excellent EMI shielding effectiveness (~48.6 dB at 1 GHz) despite low silver content (0.53 vol %).
