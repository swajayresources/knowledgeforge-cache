This paper presents the design, optimization, fabrication, and experimental validation of a compact double-layer microwave absorber for X-Ku bands (8–18 GHz) using a genetic algorithm (GA). The GA optimizes the thickness and material properties of two layers made from magneto-dielectric composites and rubber-based materials to achieve impedance matching and high absorption, resulting in reflection losses up to −28 dB at normal incidence.
