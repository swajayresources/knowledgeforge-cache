This paper presents a dual-band composite wideband absorbing material (WAM) designed to reduce the in-band radar cross section (RCS) of broadband antennas, specifically a Vivaldi antenna. The composite WAM combines a traditional absorber layer with a frequency selective surface (FSS) layer, achieving a broadened absorption bandwidth of 112% covering C and X bands with over 90% absorption.
