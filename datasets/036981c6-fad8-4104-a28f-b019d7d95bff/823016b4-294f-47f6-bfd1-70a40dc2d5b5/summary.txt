This paper revisits electromagnetic cloak design without geometric transforms by applying mono-objective and bi-objective optimization algorithms. It introduces a novel chaotic ternary grey wolf optimizer (CTGWO) for discrete ternary permittivity values and adapts a bi-objective genetic algorithm (NSGA-II) to balance two conflicting criteria: protection of a region and invisibility of the cloak.
