This paper reports the synthesis of cobalt (Co)-doped zinc oxide (ZnO) particles via the Sol-Gel method with doping concentrations of 0%, 3%, 6%, 9%, and 12%, which were embedded into styrofoam sheets to manufacture radar-absorbing composite materials. The composites exhibited enhanced electromagnetic and magnetic properties, with radar wave absorption reaching up to 93% at 10.9 GHz for the 12% Co-doped ZnO reinforced composite, demonstrating their potential for stealth technology applications.
