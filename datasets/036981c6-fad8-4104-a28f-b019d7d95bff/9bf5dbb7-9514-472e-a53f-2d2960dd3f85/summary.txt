This paper presents the design, finite element analysis, and experimental validation of adhesively bonded composite patch repairs applied to cracked 2024-T3 aluminum aircraft panels. Using octagonal single-sided boron/epoxy composite patches with 5- and 6-ply configurations, the study investigates stress distributions, strength improvement, and durability under uniaxial tensile loading.
