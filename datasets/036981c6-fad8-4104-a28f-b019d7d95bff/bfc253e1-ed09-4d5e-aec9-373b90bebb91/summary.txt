This paper presents an analytical study of low-frequency electromagnetic scattering by a perfectly conducting metal torus embedded in a lossless medium, illuminated by a time-harmonic magnetic dipole of arbitrary orientation. The scattering problem is formulated using toroidal coordinates and solved via expansions in toroidal harmonic eigenfunctions, reducing Maxwell's equations to coupled Laplace and Poisson boundary value problems.
