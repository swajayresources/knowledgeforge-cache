This paper investigates radar absorbing structures (RAS) composites filled with carbon nanotubes (CNTs) to reduce radar cross section (RCS) by absorbing electromagnetic waves. The study measures the complex permittivity of glass and carbon fiber reinforced epoxy composites with varying CNT contents using a network analyzer and optimizes multi-layer RAS designs using a genetic algorithm (GA) to maximize absorption bandwidth and peak reflection loss.
