This paper reviews the development and mechanisms of lightweight electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding materials, focusing on polymer-based composites, foams, and aerogels. It discusses EMI theory, shielding effectiveness, and the roles of reflection, absorption, and multiple reflections in shielding.
