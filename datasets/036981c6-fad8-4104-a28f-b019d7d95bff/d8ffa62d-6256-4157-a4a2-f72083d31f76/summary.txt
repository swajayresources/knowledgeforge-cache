This paper presents a method to reduce parallel-plate waveguide modes in conductor-backed slot antennas by using a two-dimensional electromagnetic bandgap (EBG) substrate. The EBG substrate suppresses unwanted backside radiation and parasitic modes, improving antenna front-to-back ratio, efficiency, and pattern quality while maintaining a low profile. Experimental and simulated results demonstrate a front-to-back ratio improvement to over 15 dB and an antenna gain of approximately 3.7 dB with 7.
