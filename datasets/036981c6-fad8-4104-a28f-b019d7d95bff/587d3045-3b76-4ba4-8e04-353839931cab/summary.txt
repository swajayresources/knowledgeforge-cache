This study investigates the microwave absorbing properties of natural ferrites extracted from iron rust corrosion products in Surabaya, Malang, and Bangkalan. The rust powders were separated using a 0.1 T hard magnet and characterized as magnetite (Fe₃O₄) with soft magnetic properties. The maximum reflection loss values for the samples were -20.15 dB (Surabaya), -12.64 dB (Malang), and -6.75 dB (Bangkalan) at a matching frequency of 11 GHz and a bandwidth of 3 GHz.
