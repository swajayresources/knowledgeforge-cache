The paper reviews the early development of stealth technology in U.S. military aviation from the 1950s to 1970, focusing on radar cross-section (RCS) reduction techniques and materials. It discusses challenges in measuring and modeling RCS, the development of radar absorber materials (RAM), and early flight demonstrations such as the RAM-covered T-33 aircraft.
