The MORCIC project develops advanced model order reduction (MOR) techniques for electromagnetic RLCk models of integrated circuits to address challenges in simulation time and memory usage. By employing low-rank balanced truncation methods accelerated with extended Krylov subspace techniques, MORCIC achieves up to 5.5× smaller reduced-order models (ROMs) than commercial tools like ANSYS RaptorX™ while maintaining comparable accuracy.
