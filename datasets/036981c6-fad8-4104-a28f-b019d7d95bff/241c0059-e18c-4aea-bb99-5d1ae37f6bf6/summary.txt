The paper presents the development of coherent FM-CW millimeter-wave radar systems operating at 35 GHz and 94 GHz for radar cross section (RCS) measurements over incidence angles from 0 to 60 degrees. These systems can operate in FM-CW mode or be converted to pulse or pulse-compression modes, and the 94 GHz system additionally functions as a total power radiometer for brightness temperature measurements.
