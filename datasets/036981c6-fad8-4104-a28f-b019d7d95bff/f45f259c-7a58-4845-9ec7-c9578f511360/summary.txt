This paper presents a novel method to reduce electromagnetic interference (EMI) from printed circuit boards (PCBs) by using electromagnetic bandgap (EBG) structures, specifically high-impedance surfaces (HIS). The technique involves embedding periodic metallo-dielectric EBG structures between power and ground planes in multilayer PCBs to suppress switching noise radiation over a wide frequency range.
