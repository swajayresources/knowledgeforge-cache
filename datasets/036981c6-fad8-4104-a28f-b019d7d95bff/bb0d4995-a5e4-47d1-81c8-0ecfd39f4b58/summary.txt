This paper presents the design and experimental demonstration of a three-dimensional (3D) invisibility cloak that conceals a macroscopic spherical object from visible light when viewed from three orthogonal directions. The cloak is realized using a novel 3D homogeneous polyhedral transformation combined with spatially invariant refractive index discretization, enabling the use of isotropic materials and simplifying fabrication.
