This paper reports the development of multifunctional polymer composites by incorporating silver nanoparticles, huntite/hydromagnesite minerals, barium hexaferrite particles, and chitosan into an epoxy matrix to achieve antibacterial, flame retardant, radar absorbing, and self-healing properties.
