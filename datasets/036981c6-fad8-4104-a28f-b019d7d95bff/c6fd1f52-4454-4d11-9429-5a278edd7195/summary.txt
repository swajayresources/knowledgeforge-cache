This paper proposes a precoding-based technique to reduce the peak-to-average power ratio (PAPR) in ASCO-OFDM and E-ASCO-OFDM visible light communication (VLC) systems. The authors introduce precoding matrices such as DHT, DCT, DST, and VLM, achieving significant PAPR reduction without degrading bit error rate (BER) performance. The proposed PE-ASCO-OFDM system demonstrates improved power efficiency and spectral efficiency compared to conventional approaches.
