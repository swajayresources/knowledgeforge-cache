This paper investigates the effects of electron and neutron irradiation on the electromagnetic interference shielding effectiveness (EMI-SE) and conductivity of multi-walled carbon nanotube (MWCNT) composites, which are being considered for use as satellite surface materials. The results show that the layered configuration of the MWCNT composites plays a role in their durability under irradiation, with the configuration having two outer layers of MWCNT performing the best.
