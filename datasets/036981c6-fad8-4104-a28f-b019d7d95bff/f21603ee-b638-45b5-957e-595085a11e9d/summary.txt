The paper chronicles the early development of stealth technology in U.S. military aviation, focusing on radar cross section (RCS) reduction methods from the 1950s through the 1970s. It details the challenges of measuring and modeling radar echoes, the development of radar-absorbent materials (RAM), and the first flight demonstrations of stealth concepts using RAM-coated aircraft.
