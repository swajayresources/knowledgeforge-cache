This research investigates the fabrication, characterization, and sound absorption performance of a composite material made from water hyacinth biochar (WHBC) and polyvinyl alcohol (PVA). The WHBC-PVA composite, produced by pyrolyzing water hyacinth at different temperatures and blending with PVA, demonstrated superior sound absorption coefficients (up to 21.03%) compared to conventional materials like Styrofoam, especially at low frequencies around 440 Hz.
