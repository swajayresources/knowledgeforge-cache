This study developed a PSMA-PET-guided CT-based radiomic signature to predict biochemical recurrence (BCR) after salvage radiotherapy (sRT) in prostate cancer patients. The radiomic model outperformed clinical and combined clinical-radiomic models, achieving a C-index of 0.71 compared to 0.53 and 0.63, respectively, and demonstrated significant patient stratification in Kaplan-Meier analysis.
