This paper proposes a design method for a two-dimensional broadband free-space electromagnetic cloak using isotropic materials based on quasi-conformal mapping (QCM). The method optimally scales the refractive index profile to compensate for anisotropy and size reduction effects, achieving up to 78.3% size reduction and 71% reduction in normalized scattering cross section (SCS) at 10 GHz.
