This paper investigates the effect of turbulence on the dynamic Radar Cross Section (RCS) of chaff clouds used as electronic countermeasures in modern warfare. Using computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations with ANSYS Fluent and electromagnetic simulations with ANSYS HFSS, the study shows that turbulence enhances the rapid blooming and dispersion of chaff filaments, increasing RCS by 1.5 to 2.5 dBsm within 50 milliseconds and reducing blooming time by 25 to 50 milliseconds.
