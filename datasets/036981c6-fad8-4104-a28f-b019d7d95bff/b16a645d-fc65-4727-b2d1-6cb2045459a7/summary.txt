This paper proposes DKSAP-IoT, a faster dual-key stealth address protocol designed for blockchain-based Internet of Things (IoT) systems. By leveraging a key-evolving technique inspired by TLS session resumption, DKSAP-IoT reduces computational overhead and transaction size significantly, making stealth address privacy protection feasible for resource-constrained IoT devices. Experimental results on embedded platforms show at least 50% performance improvement compared to the original DKSAP.
