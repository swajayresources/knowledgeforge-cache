This paper reports the first experimental realization of a macroscopic invisibility cloak operating at visible light frequencies. The cloak, made from two pieces of natural birefringent calcite crystals, can conceal a macroscopic object up to 2 mm tall (over 3500 wavelengths) inside a transparent liquid environment, working across red, green, and blue light.
