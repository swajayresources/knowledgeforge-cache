This comprehensive review by F. Michael Kahnert examines the theoretical foundations and practical applications of major numerical methods for solving electromagnetic scattering problems, including differential equation methods (SVM, FDTD, FEM, MoL, PMM), volume-integral equation methods (MoM, DDA), and surface-integral equation methods (NFM).
