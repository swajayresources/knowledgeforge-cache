This study used a bias reduction small RNA deep sequencing protocol to profile miRNA expression in 44 paired colorectal cancer (CRC) and normal tissue samples. The results revealed that miR-21 and miR-143 are the two most highly expressed miRNAs in CRC, together comprising over 60% of the total miRNA population. The study also identified 8 other highly expressed miRNAs in CRC and validated their dysregulation in CRC cell lines.
