The paper reports the synthesis of Fe3O4/carbon composite nanofibers via electrospinning of polyacrylonitrile (PAN)/acetylacetone iron (AAI)/DMF solution, followed by stabilization and carbonization. These composite nanofibers exhibit enhanced microwave absorption performance compared to pure carbon nanofibers, showing lower reflection loss values, wider absorption bandwidths (up to 12–13 GHz under −5 dB), and improved low-frequency absorption (2–5 GHz).
