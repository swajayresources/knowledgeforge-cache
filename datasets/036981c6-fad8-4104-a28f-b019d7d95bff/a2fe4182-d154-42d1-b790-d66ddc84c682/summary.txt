This paper presents the development of an active vision-based space-positioning robot (AVBSPR) for surgical navigation, utilizing a five-axis SCROBOT-ER VII robot equipped with stereoscopic image capture and infrared (IR) surgical marker emitters. The system integrates fuzzy logic-based image servo tracking to dynamically track moving surgical markers and compute their 3D coordinates, achieving a positioning error within 5 mm over a 2000 mm operating range.
