This paper investigates how the geometric parameters of a single-gap split-ring resonator (SRR) metamaterial unit cell affect its electromagnetic properties, particularly the effective magnetic permeability, in the microwave regime around 9.5 GHz. The study uses simulation and theoretical analysis to optimize SRR designs for use as unit cells in a ten-layer cylindrical interior invisibility cloak operating at microwave frequencies.
