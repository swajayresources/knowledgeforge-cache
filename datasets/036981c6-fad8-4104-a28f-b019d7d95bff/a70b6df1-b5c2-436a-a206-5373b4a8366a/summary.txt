This paper investigates electromagnetic wave propagation in unmagnetized plasmas and its application to reducing the radar cross-section (RCS) of a plasma-filled enclosure. The authors demonstrate RCS reduction of 20 to 25 dB using a prototype system and develop a theoretical model for the plasma cloaking process. The model includes scattering contributions from the plasma-vacuum interface, partial reflections from the plasma, and collisional absorption in the plasma.
