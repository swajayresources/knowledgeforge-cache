This paper presents the fabrication and evaluation of three-dimensional hybrid composite radar absorbing structures (RAS) in 'C' and 'U' shapes. The RAS shells combine glass fiber/epoxy/carbon-black (absorbing layer) and carbon fiber/epoxy (reflection layer), with spring-back compensation achieved using aluminum molds. The hybrid RAS demonstrated an average radar absorption efficiency of 11 dB, with experimental and predicted radar cross section (RCS) results showing good agreement.
