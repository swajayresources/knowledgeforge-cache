This paper proposes an improved active infrared (IR) stealth scheme called the inverse distance weighted active IR stealth method, which synchronizes the IR signals of an object with its surrounding background by minimizing the inverse distance weighted contrast radiant intensity (CRI) using a repulsive particle swarm optimization (RPSO) algorithm.
