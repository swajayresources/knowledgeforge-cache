This paper presents the first experimental demonstration of an invisibility cloak printed directly on a photonic chip using femtosecond laser direct writing. The cloak consists of up to 5,000 three-dimensionally arrayed waveguides in borosilicate glass that guide light around a millimeter-scale hidden structure, achieving cloaking by manipulating light propagation paths with collective curvature.
