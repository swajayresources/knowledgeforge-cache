This paper presents the development of two novel X-band metamaterial radar absorbers: a fan shaped split ring resonator (SRR) absorber and a modified high impedance ground plane (HIGP) absorber. Both designs achieve wider bandwidths and higher absorption levels compared to conventional rectangular SRR and patch absorbers, while maintaining performance for both co-polarized and cross-polarized incident waves.
