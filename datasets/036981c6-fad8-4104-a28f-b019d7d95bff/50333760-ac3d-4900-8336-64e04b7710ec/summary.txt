This paper investigates the absorption characteristics of a low-cost composite electromagnetic wave absorber made of spherical sendust particles dispersed in polystyrene resin, targeting frequencies above 10 GHz. The study finds that optimal volume ratios of approximately 30 vol% for 5 µm particles and 35 vol% for 20 µm particles maximize the absorption bandwidth, which is explained by the combined effects of natural magnetic resonance and eddy currents on the relative complex permeability.
