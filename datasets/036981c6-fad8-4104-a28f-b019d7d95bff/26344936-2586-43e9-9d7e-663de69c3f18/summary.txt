This study presents a comparative experimental methodology to characterize the radar cross-section area (RSA) reduction efficiency and performance of elastomer-based radar absorber materials (H-RGCs) prepared using nano-sized conductive fillers in the 6-17 GHz frequency band. The results show that the H-RGC material can achieve a reduction of up to 20 dBsm, comparable to the commercially available reference material (M-REM).
