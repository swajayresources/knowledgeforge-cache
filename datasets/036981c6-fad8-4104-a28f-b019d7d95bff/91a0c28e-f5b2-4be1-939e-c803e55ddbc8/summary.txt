BaseSAP is a modular, open, and reusable stealth address protocol designed for programmable blockchains like Ethereum, enabling users to generate unique, unlinkable stealth addresses at the application layer without modifying the core blockchain. It builds on prior stealth address research, particularly Dual-Key Stealth Address Protocols (DKSAP), and supports extensibility with different cryptographic schemes, demonstrated by a Secp256k1-based dual-key prototype deployed on Ethereum testnets.
