This review by Susan Gottesman examines small regulatory RNAs (sRNAs) as a previously overlooked level of gene regulation in both prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells. These 'stealth regulators' control translation and degradation of messenger RNAs through two main mechanisms: antisense pairing with target RNAs and molecular mimicry of protein targets.
