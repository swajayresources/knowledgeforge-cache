This paper investigates the terahertz (160 GHz) backscattering behavior of various radar absorbing materials (RAMs) using a compact radar range. Seven different absorbers, including wedged, pyramidal, and bulk types, were characterized for their radar cross section (RCS) and normal incidence transmittance across a range of elevation and azimuth angles. The study reveals distinct scattering behaviors related to absorber geometry and material composition, providing insights into their performance
