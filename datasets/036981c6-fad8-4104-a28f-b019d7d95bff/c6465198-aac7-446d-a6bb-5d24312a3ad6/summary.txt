This paper investigates the reduction of antenna radar cross-section (RCS) to enhance stealth technology by designing and analyzing low-observable microstrip patch antennas operating between 2 and 12 GHz. Using CST Microwave Studio, three antenna models were developed incorporating geometric shaping, radar absorbing materials (RAM), and frequency selective surfaces (FSS), achieving up to 80% RCS reduction while maintaining communication performance such as bandwidth, gain, and directivity.
