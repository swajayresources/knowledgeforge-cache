This paper investigates the reduction of antenna radar cross-section (RCS) to enhance stealth technology by designing low-observable microstrip patch antennas operating in the 2–12 GHz range. Using CST Microwave Studio simulations, three antenna models were developed incorporating geometric shaping, radar absorbing materials (RAM), and frequency selective surfaces (FSS) to achieve up to 80% RCS reduction while maintaining communication performance.
