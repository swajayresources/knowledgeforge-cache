This paper proposes a two-timescale (TTS) optimization framework for movable antenna (MA) enabled integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems to address practical challenges such as slow antenna movement, dynamic radar cross section (RCS) fluctuations, and imperfect channel state information (CSI). The framework jointly optimizes discrete MA positions, beamforming vectors, and snapshot durations to minimize base station transmit power while guaranteeing sensing and communication quality
