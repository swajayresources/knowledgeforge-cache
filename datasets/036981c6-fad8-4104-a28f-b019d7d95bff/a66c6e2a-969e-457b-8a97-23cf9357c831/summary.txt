This paper presents a thin, single-layer broadband radar absorber based on a co-polarization Underlined U-Shaped Resonator (UUSR) designed for radar cross section (RCS) reduction. The absorber achieves over 90% absorption from 5.6 GHz to 9.1 GHz for both TE and TM polarizations at normal incidence, with a thickness 12.7 times thinner than the wavelength at the center frequency.
