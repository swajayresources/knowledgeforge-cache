This paper reports the successful realization of a human-sized, wideband, high-fidelity microwave invisibility cloak using fractal metamaterials arranged in two cloaking layers around a copper cylindrical mirror. The cloak achieves volumetric cloaking of a person over a bandwidth exceeding 50%, demonstrating substantial re-attainment of the direct path microwave intensity and overcoming previous limitations of size, bandwidth, and fidelity in invisibility cloaks.
