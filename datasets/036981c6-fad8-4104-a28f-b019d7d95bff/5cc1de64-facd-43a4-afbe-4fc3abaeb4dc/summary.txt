This paper develops an analytical scattering method for electromagnetic wave interaction with radially anisotropic, inhomogeneous spherical cloaks having arbitrary radial-dependent permittivity and permeability parameters. It proposes a general cloaking condition based on wave relations rather than transformation optics and introduces a novel class of nonlinear-transformation-based spherical cloaks that demonstrate improved invisibility performance compared to Pendry's classic linear cloak.
