This paper develops and evaluates a versatile semi-active suspension system combining variable stiffness (VS) magnetorheological elastomer (MRE) isolators and variable damping (VD) magnetorheological (MR) dampers for high-speed railway vehicles. The system uses a short-time Fourier transform (STFT) and sky-hook control strategy to avoid lateral resonance and dissipate vibration energy, demonstrating superior performance compared to passive and single-function suspension systems.
