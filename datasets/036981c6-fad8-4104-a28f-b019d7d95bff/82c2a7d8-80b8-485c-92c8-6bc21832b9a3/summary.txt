This paper investigates how the particle size of sendust (an alloy of Al 5%, Si 10%, Fe 85%) affects the absorption characteristics of composite electromagnetic wave absorbers made with polystyrene resin in the 1 to 40 GHz frequency range. It finds that larger particles (20 µm) achieve over 99% absorption above 20 GHz, while smaller particles (5 µm) provide broad bandwidth absorption including below 10 GHz and above 30 GHz.
